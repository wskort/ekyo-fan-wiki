# Dr Horrible's Sing-Along Blog
## Act I
Dr Horrible talks straight to the camera, blogging his efforts in becoming a big-time villain instead of just a small fry. His previous results have been... mixed at best. But his new plan will be great! He's building a freeze ray, as in pause, not ice. 

In his daily life, Billy is socially awkward and very reserved. He has been pining for a girl he sees twice a week at the laundry mat for months, but has never actually spoken to her yet. He fantasizes about using his freeze ray to buy time to find the words to actually talk to her. The first song goes back and forth between Billy glancing at her and Dr Horrible bragging to his audience about his newest invention.

Dr Horrible's friend Moist enters and Dr Horrible cuts the blog. Dr Horrible receives a response from the Evil League of Evil: they'll review his application this one time but if he doesn't pull off something big, he's out for good. Not a problem, Dr Horrible has a plan lined up. 

We see Billy out in the street setting up his heist. He attaches a magnetized remote control to a courier van that will transport some material he needs for his gun, and links up his phone app to control it. But then the girl actually comes up to him to gather signatures for an expansion for the local homeless centre. She recognizes him from the laundry mat. Billy lets her do the speech but is very much distracted, watching for the delivery to be made, and when she *does* get his attention he flaps out some flawed rhetoric about overhauling the system instead of fighting the disease, panics, then signs her petition. The moment the material is in view, he ignores her entirely and she leaves. 

"She talked to me! Why did she talk to me *now*?"

He changes into the Dr Horrible costume while singing to himself to stick to the plan and takes control of the van. 

But what's that!

Captain Hammer jumps onto the van, takes over the song, and smashes the remote control. Unfortunately, this means that now *no one* controls it, and it's about to run over Penny. While Dr Horrible frantically tries to regain control of the van, Captain Hammer pushes her out of the way into a large pile of trash and makes to stop the van, which finally connects to the phone and stops right before hitting Hammer. Penny is grateful of Hammer for saving her life, while Dr Horrible in the background sings that Hammer almost killed her and Dr Horrible stopped the van and and and... He takes the science material and retreats. 

## Act II
In the next song, Billy is losing hope for humanity while Penny is gaining hope for humanity. We see Billy stalking Penny while she goes on a date with Captain Hammer, taking him to the homeless shelter she volunteers at. Captain Hammer is clearly uncomfortable with the environment, playing along to gain her affection. 

Billy and Penny meet again at the laundry mat and Billy is pretending to agree with all her opinions, being friendly, and then asks about her weekend, knowing about her date with Captain Hammer. She tells him about the date, and Billy wastes no time starting to badmouth Hammer. He tries to stay friendly and optimistic, but is stabbing himself with a spork to deal with the agony. 

Blog time: the freeze ray is finished, but he has "inadvertently introduced my arch-nemesis to the girl of my dreams, and now he's taking her out on dates, and they're probably going to french kiss or something."

Next blog: Dr Horrible is battered and bruised. Note to self: don't announce the details of his schemes, since Captain Hammer follows his blog. He gets a call from Bad Horse while still recording: he saw that disaster of an operation and now murder is the only way to qualify for membership in the Evil League of Evil. 

Off-blog, Dr Horrible talks with Moist about this development. He still feels he deserves to be in the league and scoffs at the "Henchman's Union", but is conflicted about murder. "Killing's not elegant or creative. It's not my style."

In the laundry mat, Billy is venting about it to Penny as "a job" that he feels he deserves but can't seem to get. She is encouraging, and sings a short song about her own past struggles, and how in the darkest times she finds hope by helping those that are even worse off; this is how she got to volunteering in the homeless shelter. She then tells Billy that Captain Hammer might stop by soon, and Billy tries to bolt but it's too late, Hammer is here, and recognizes Billy as Dr Horrible but doesn't out him to Penny. They make small talk and Hammer brags that he talked to the mayor and fast-tracked the expansion for the homeless shelter. Penny is ecstatic, but as soon as she steps away, Captain Hammer bullies "Doc" to say that he's dating Penny just because Doc likes her, and he's going to fuck her. 

Next song: Billy has solved his dilemma. He's going to murder Captain Hammer. Sure, Penny will cry, but she'll get over it. 

## Act III
We start with an ensemble piece about how Captain Hammer has become more politically engaged, helping Penny set up that new homeless shelter, and how the public and Hammer's fan club perceive their relationship. We also get a hint that Penny isn't sure about the relationship and feels pressured by the outside appearance of fairy tale happiness, while Captain Hammer muses about the sexual benefits of long term relationships. Everyone is preparing for the grand opening of the new shelter, including Dr Horrible who has upgraded his stun ray to a death ray. 

Opening ceremony. Mayor introduces Captain Hammer to the stage, who sings about how his love for Penny inspired him to help people beyond just beating up bad guys. The public is lapping it up, but during the speech Penny awkwardly sneaks off stage. 

Dr Horrible emerges from under a red curtain that was supposed to hold a statue of Captain Hammer and freezes Captain Hammer. He sing-speeches to the crowd, corrects a journalist spelling his name, and is relieved to notice that Penny isn't on stage. He doesn't see her hiding behind a row of chairs. But he takes too long trying to hype himself up to actually fire the death ray at Captain Hammer, and the freeze ray runs out. Captain Hammer punches him to the ground, takes his gun, and uses the gun to shoot a protesting Dr Horrible. The gun explodes, throwing Captain Hammer across the room and shooting shrapnel all over the place. Dr Horrible is unharmed. Captain Hammer experiences pain for the first time in his life and runs away sobbing in terror. But Penny has been hit right in the chest with big shards of shrapnel. In her dazed state, she recognizes Billy but doesn't put together that he's Dr Horrible. She asks if *he* is alright. Billy is terrified, looks around for a doctor, but Penny says "It's okay. Captain Hammer will save us." Then dies. 

Billy is still reeling when the press begin to take his picture and demand to know why Dr Horrible killed her. He sings his evil victory while looking dazed, reframing on the fly, and lays her body on a stretcher. "And I am fine." Heists are much easier after this due to his increased reputation. The city mourns 'what's her face' and Captain Hammer is shown blubbering to a shrink. Hammer's fan club starts to idolize Dr Horrible. Later in his lab, there's a party full of villains. Dr Horrible upgrades his suit and he joins the Evil League of Evil. "And I won't feel" -- cut to *Billy* blogging with a haunted expression "A thing."
