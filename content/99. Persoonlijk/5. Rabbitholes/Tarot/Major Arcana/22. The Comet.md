# 22. The Comet

## Light: 
Epiphany, inspiration, transformation, brilliance, rapid progress, divine guidance, opportunity, clarity.

## Shadow: 
Overwhelm, instability, missed chances, impulsiveness, chaos, fleeting success, burnout, distraction.
