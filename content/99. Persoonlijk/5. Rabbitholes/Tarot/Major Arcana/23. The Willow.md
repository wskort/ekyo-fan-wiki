# 23. The Willow

## Light: 
Resilience, adaptability, wisdom, perseverance, connection to nature, grounding, quiet strength, healing.

## Shadow: 
Oppression, overcontrol, emotional suppression, fragility, dependency, stagnation, isolation, insecurity
