# 24. The Artisan

## Light: 
Creativity, skill, innovation, mastery, dedication, craftsmanship, resourcefulness, self-expression.

## Shadow: 
Perfectionism, self-doubt, burnout, stagnation, obsession, overwork, superficiality, lack of recognition.
