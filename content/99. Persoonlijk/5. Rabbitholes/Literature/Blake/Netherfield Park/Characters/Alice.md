# Alice ([[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] / Neo)
- Formerly unhoused orphan
- Traumatically re-educated after arrest for something petty, like stealing a bread from a street vendor
- Reassigned work as an "elder carer": reading books to clients in a retirement home
- Discovered and fell in love with old fiction, crucially [[Jane Austen]]
- Superfan; deck out the apartment like in Austenland, cardboard Harley and all.
- Won a [[0. Bronze|Bronze]]-level ticket in a special lottery
- *Wants* a storybook romance to remember and be able to enjoy their life as a dead-end elder carer
- *Needs* to be empowered to actually develop their skills and better their life
