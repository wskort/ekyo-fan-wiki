FADE IN:

Ext. IN FRONT OF CASTLE - NIGHT

We OPEN on a small castle in the English countryside. There are two smaller houses to either side of the entrance plaza. The castle is lit from inside, and there seems to be some kind of reception going on. The two houses are minimally lit.

We push in closer: A group of CHATTERING WOMEN is being led from the left house to the castle entrance by MR. PRICE (30s, stern black-clad man).

MR Price
	Come along, ladies! The gentlemen are eager to make your acquaintance.

Some of the women chatter about their excitement and fantasies about the eligible bachelors they're about to meet.

One of them mentions an older sister who went to an earlier ball and came home married to a count, and another adds that her niece expecting her first child with the young man she met there last year.

Then DUCHESS ANDIE (early 20's, extravagant dress, platinum blonde in elaborate updo) and BLAKE (early 20's, plain dress, long sleek dark hair in simple bun) walk into view. BLAKE looks enraptured with the beauty and elegance of the environment, but ANDIE looks annoyed.

ANDIE
	(quiet)
	You shouldn't even be here.

Blake
	(demure)
	It was exceedingly kind of LADY MAL to allow me to accompany you, your Grace.

ANDIE
	I can't believe she invited my grandmother's ward. No one wants some penniless orphan traipsing around.

ANDIE continues to taunt BLAKE, who takes it all with a downcast look. She seems to have internalized it all.

ANDIE beams a bright smile at someone off-camera as they enter the castle, already neglecting BLAKE.

INT. CASTLE ENTRANCE - NIGHT

SERVANTS are lined to either side of the entrance hall and LADY MAL DE COURCY (50s, overly made up, height of fashion) greets the incoming ladies with open arms and a broad smile.

LADY MAL
	Welcome, welcome! I'm so glad you could all make it. Ah, such lovely young ladies you are. You are sure to turn a few heads, yes indeed!

A few of the ladies titter in excitement and ANDIE radiates pride and arrogance. BLAKE stays at the back.

LADY MAL
	The grand ball is in just two days, so make the best of your time to get to know each other. As you know, my annual balls are known to inspire romance...

Ladies
	(whooping)

Lady mal
	But! I of course expect you to maintain _proper decorum_. We wouldn't want to have to rush any of your upcoming nuptuals, now would we? Although I'm sure that Reverend Hearst would be more than happy to oblige any particularly _impatient_ young lovers.

LADY MAL observes the gathered women. Most look excited, and at least one seems lustful. BLAKE shrinks away from her inspecting look.

Lady MAL
	Now, I won't hold you in suspense any longer. Let the mingling begin!

Two SERVANTS open the double doors to the CASTLE PARLOUR.

INT. CASTLE PARLOUR - night

In the next room, more SERVANTS are waiting with trays of champagne. Further in, various GENTLEMEN are standing in small conversational groups.

The WOMEN eagerly spread through the estate, introducing themselves to the charming gentlemen.

ANDIE gives BLAKE one last sideways glance as they both enter the room.

ANDIE
	(whispered)
	Stay out of my way.

ANDIE is almost immediately approached by three gentlemen competing for her attention, and she laughs animatedly as she lets them lead her further inside.

BLAKE looks overwhelmed. She hangs back, sticks to the wall, and clutches her drink like it will defend her. We follow her gaze: she looks at the entrance she just came from, then spots ANDIE getting shown to a room further inside, and finally a servants' door where a SERVANT enters with a plate of appetizers.

The SERVANT offers BLAKE an appetizer, which she shyly declines. The SERVANT moves on, and BLAKE inches closer to the servants' door.

While BLAKE is watching her exit strategy, we see that across the room MR THOMAS HALES (late 20's, blond, athletic, dressed in light colours and with a large baby blue bow around his neck) is watching BLAKE with interest. He is twirling his drink and he smirks darkly. His eyes are those of a predator.
