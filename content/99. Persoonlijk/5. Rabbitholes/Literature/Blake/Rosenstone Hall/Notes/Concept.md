reimagining of [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroines/3. Bronze/Blake|Earnest]]x[[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Hales]] with added backstory and realized ending. The admiral that took in [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] took this young pickpocket in off the streets, deciding to give this orphan streetrat some much needed structure and discipline, and eventually adopting him to be his heir, was also the officer of the law that caught young [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] in an attempt to deliver a message from a career criminal (think Fagin from Oliver Twist). Since the Admiral didn’t think that putting these kids together would do either of them any good, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] was sent to a strict orphanage instead. Not long after, the cruel matron took [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] to see the career criminal’s public hanging. A few months of intense abuse and neglect later, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] was quite broken down and hollowed out. This is when the [[Andie|Springs]] family took her in. The story opens as before, with the [[Andie|Duchess]] and [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Blake]] arriving a few days before the ball; backstories are slowly teased out. [[Mr Frank Hartford|Mr Hartford]] is presented as "first man" but psych, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] is waaaay earlier.

This one stays fairly true to events as they unfolded within [[Miss Earnest's journal]], deviating at Hide & Seek (no predatory [[Quinn|Miss Winters]]) and continuing from there. But make [[Andie|Duchess Springs]] as cruel as prescribed, instead of an awkward softie, and replace [[Daya]] with [[Andie]] for the stolen dance.
