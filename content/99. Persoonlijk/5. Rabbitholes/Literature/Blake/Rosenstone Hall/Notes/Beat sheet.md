# **ROSENSTONE HALL**

# ACT I — ARRIVAL & MISREADING
_(What the reader thinks the story is)_

## Chapter 1 — Longbourn Park

**Present**
- Blake and Duchess Andie arrive early.
- Blake observes the estate, routes, rooms, exits.
- She is already positioning herself as useful.
- Mr Hartford is introduced: gentle, attentive, appropriate.

**Flashback 1 (brief, fragmentary)**
- A child counting doorways, timing routes.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Blake is disciplined, observant. Nothing alarming yet.

## Chapter 2 — The Duchess’s Shadow

**Present**
- Blake assists Andie flawlessly.
- Andie’s “kindness” contains faint contempt.
- Blake absorbs it without reaction.

**Flashback 2**
- A man’s voice: _“If you hesitate, you’re dead.”_

**Reader takeaway:**  
Blake responds to pressure with competence, not protest.

## Chapter 3 — The Letter

**Present**
- Kamryn’s letter exercise.
- Blake writes modestly, honestly.
- Leaves the letter behind; it goes unnoticed.

**Flashback 3**
- Blake as a girl delivering messages.
- The Broker checks her hands afterward.
    - Not gentle.
    - Not cruel.
    - Exact.

**Reader takeaway:**  
She has been trained, not merely raised.

## Chapter 4 — Mr Hartford

**Present**
- Walks, poetry, quiet companionship.
- A plausible, safe future is sketched.

**Flashback 4**
- The Broker saying:  
    _“Safe is a story people tell after they’ve survived.”_

**Reader takeaway:**  
Hartford feels right — but Blake doesn’t relax.

## Chapter 5 — The Warning

**Present**
- Cpt. Graham warns Blake against Mr Hales.
- He offers no explanation, only concern.

**Flashback 5**
- A uniformed man saying:  
    _“This is for your own good.”_
- Immediate cut to the Broker:  
    _“No one does things for your good.”_

**Reader takeaway:**  
Blake trusts brutal honesty over benevolence.

## Chapter 6 — First Encounter with Hales

**Present**
- Verbal sparring: games, chases, agency.
- Chemistry is immediate, unsettling.

**Flashback 6**
- Blake being searched by authority.
- Calm, methodical.
- Afterwards, the Broker shielding her from consequences.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Hales triggers familiarity — not attraction alone.

## Chapter 7 — The Stolen Dance

**Present**
- Blake steals Hales from Andie.
- Andie’s offense is jurisdictional, not jealous.
- Blake instantly realizes she has overstepped.

**Flashback 7**
- Blake being removed from a room without explanation.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Blake knows what displacement feels like — and its cost.

# ACT II — PATTERNS & DISTURBANCE
_(What the reader begins to understand)_

## Chapter 8 — Withdrawal

**Present**
- Blake engineers distance.
- Leads dances as a gentleman.
- Hales adapts; interest intensifies.

**Flashback 8**
- The Broker teaching her when to disappear.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Engagement invites scrutiny. Exit preserves control.

## Chapter 9 — Dinner

**Present**
- Seating forces proximity.
- Knees brush.
- Hales escalates without malice.
- Blake jolts and leaves the table.

**Flashback 9**
- A bell ringing.
- Immediate movement required.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Her body responds before her mind.

## Chapter 10 — Competence

**Present**
- Blake serves her entire group when food is missed.
- Authority through action.

**Flashback 10**
- The Broker praising her efficiency:  
    _“You’re alive because you don’t wait.”_

**Reader takeaway:**  
Usefulness is survival, not virtue.


## Chapter 11 — Hales’s Story (Partial)

**Present**
- Hales shares his childhood:
    - orphaned
    - taken in by an Admiral
    - structure saved him

**No flashback.**

**Reader takeaway:**  
This explains _him_ — but not _her_.


## Chapter 12 — The Orphanage

**Flashback Chapter (first full reveal)**
- Blake is taken from the streets.
- Delivered to the orphanage.
- The Broker is named as a criminal influence.
- She searches for him — he is already unreachable.

**Present frame**
- Blake watching couples laugh.

**Reader takeaway:**  
She was institutionalized after losing the only person who claimed her.


## Chapter 13 — Hide & Seek

**Present**
- Blake agrees to play.
- Paired with Hales.
- He escalates, assuming consent.
- She freezes — then disengages.

**Flashback 11**
- Stillness as survival.

**Reader takeaway:**  
This is not flirtation. It’s a misread of control.


## Chapter 14 — The Exit

**Present**
- Blake leaves the game early.
- Hales senses rupture.

**Flashback 12**
- The crowd gathering.
- A scaffold being erected.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Something irreversible is approaching.


# ACT III — CAUSE & CONSEQUENCE
_(What everything has always been about)_


## Chapter 15 — The Hanging

**Flashback Chapter (central trauma)**
- The matron takes Blake to the execution.
- The Broker is publicly hanged.
- Framed as moral instruction.
- Blake understands:
    - he is being destroyed for standing outside the system
    - no replacement will be offered

**Reader takeaway:**  
Protection is annihilated when it cannot be absorbed.


## Chapter 16 — After the Scaffold

**Flashback**
- Blake returns to the orphanage.
- Silence.
- No one claims her.
- No one even knows her well enough to punish.

**Present frame**
- Blake navigating Longbourn rules flawlessly.

**Reader takeaway:**  
This is where she learns to disappear _before_ harm.


## Chapter 17 — Andie’s Teeth

**Present**
- Andie’s cruelty sharpens.
- Subtle threats of disposability.
- Blake plans distance.

**Flashback 13**
- The Springs family taking Blake in.
- Conditional kindness.
- Gratitude demanded.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Rescue always comes with enclosure.


## Chapter 18 — The Admiral Named

**Present**
- Blake reveals the Admiral to Hales.
- The shared past clicks into place.

**Flashback 14**
- The Admiral separating the children.
- Choosing absorption over annihilation.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Hales survived by being usable.


## Chapter 19 — Escalation

**Present**
- Passion intensifies.
- Blake keeps exiting.
- Hales grows desperate.

**No flashback.**

**Reader takeaway:**  
All defenses are now active.


## Chapter 20 — The Proposal

**Present**
- Hales proposes marriage.
- Framed as safety, certainty, legitimacy.
- Blake does not answer.

**Reader takeaway:**  
He is offering the very structure that destroyed her protector.


## Chapter 21 — The Night

**Present**
- Blake chooses intimacy without resolution.
- No promises.
- No future named.

**Reader takeaway:**  
Desire reclaimed without enclosure.


## Chapter 22 — Morning

**Present**
- Ordinary details.
- Blake leaves.

**Flashback 15 (final)**
- Young Blake running freely through the streets.

**Reader takeaway:**  
She returns to motion, not escape.


## Chapter 23 — Aftermath (Hales)

**Present**
- Hales wakes to absence.
- Understands too late what he offered.


## Chapter 24 — Coda

**Present**
- Blake elsewhere.
- Writing.
- Observing.
- Unclaimed.
