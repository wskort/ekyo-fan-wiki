# The Water of my life

Water surrounds me.

It covers me all over.

All I can feel now is the cold, smooth water, holding me in its sweet embrace.

Water has become a part of me.

And if I don't get out of here soon, I'll become a part of the water, too.

Everywhere I look, there is water.

It's practically a miracle I can still hold my head up.

My feat can't reach the bottom, and my eyes can't reach the shore.

I guess I should be thankfull I haven't drowned already.

I don't cry for help.

I know there's no one there to save me.

But one can always hope, right?

I don't know where I am or how I got here, but I suppose it doesn't matter.

All that matters now is that my legs won't swim much longer.

I wasn't trying to swim anywhere.

I wouldn't know where to, anyway.

I'm just figuring that sooner or later someone will pass by.

I honestly can't tell what kept me afloat for so long.

Somehow, it doesn't seem to be working any more. And I'm thankfull to still be alive, I honestly am.

I just don't know if it'll do anybody good.

I'm so tired...

I just want... to... rest...

No! I cannot rest! I can't stop now!

I can't let years of fighting simply go to waste!

Still, I'd give anything for a boat, a raft, a log even.

Just a little break...

I still take no innitiative.

I simply keep on swimming.

And I don't call for help.

After all, there's no one here to hear me. Right?

... Right?

It would only waste my energy.

I cannot take the chance.

For if my call will not be anwsered, my lonelyness will be a fact.

Even more than it is now.

Because that is what I am.

Alone.

Unwanted.

Carelessly tossed overboard.

Without a life jacket in the open sea.

I can't hold much longer.

I think I'm about to drown...

Drown in the deep...

Blue...

Ocean...

Of my heart...

Farewell, dear friend of mine.

You were never meant to save me.

The ocean was.

The ocean will save me.

After all, many have drowned before me.

I'm sure we'll get along.

So farewell, and may it take a hell of a long time before you choose to join me...
