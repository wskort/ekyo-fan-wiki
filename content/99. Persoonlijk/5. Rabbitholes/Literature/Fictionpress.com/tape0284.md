# tape0284

I can't sleep. It just isn't the right time for it, you know? … To be honest, I haven't slept for over 72 hours. But I'm not tired, really. Now's just not the time to go to sleep. I suppose I <em>could</em> try and take a nap… But it'd probably last a bit longer than a nap, and I might be out for over 12 hours… And that would be disaster. I can't just go to sleep for 12 hours, now, can I? They might need me… They might call. If I'd go to sleep now, I'd be so out of it, I wouldn't even hear the phone… And that, that… That would <em>definitely</em> be disaster. So here I am, aren't I? I must be stupid, talking into a tape-recorder, but I guess that's just my way of coping. Knowing that they're out there, doing god knows what, and I'm stuck in here… I can't honestly say I'm tired, but talking really does help. It almost feels like there's someone on the other side, you know? Which is stupid… I mean, I'm not even talking on a dead radio frequency, it's a <em>tape-recorder</em>, for crying out loud! When all this is over, I'm probably not even going to listen to this anymore… In fact, I'd probably have to tape over it as <em>fast</em> as I can, before the gang finds this thing and uses it to torture me for the rest of my life, haha! … Unless… Unless we don't… But no, I can't think like that right now. We're in the midst of everything, now is really not the time to think thoughts like that, let alone speak it. … I wish they'd call in. I wish they'd let me in on what's happening. Why can't they call? Are they in trouble? Or… Or did they just forget about me? That'd be rich… They could be laughing about the whole deal in a bar this very moment, drinking a nice chug of beer and laughing away the fear they must've felt… And forgetting all about the fear <em>I'm</em> going through, the fear of the one who's left behind… I shouldn't be jealous, I know. They're out there, risking their lives (or drinking that beer after all, I don't know) and I don't even go near danger. It's not that I chicken out or something, I'm just… Needed here. The getaway-car, sort to speak. Of course, it's <em>technically</em> not a car, it's a jet… But still, you know?
… I wish <em>someone</em> would call me. Let me in on what's happening, you know? Then again, Kirsten <em>did</em> tell me to take a nap… Maybe they think I'm sleeping… Yeah, that'll be it. They just don't want to wake me up… But no, that's nothing like them. It's nothing like them to remember what they said to me, or what they <em>didn't</em> say to me. They're always forgetting about me… Always assume me to be fully up to date, which I obviously never am… Almost never, anyway.
… They're taking a really long time now, aren't they?

He pushes the pause button, his fingers tightening and eyes closing in a forced kind of way. So this is how she felt…this were her thoughts before…before…He swallows and opens his eyes. All around him he sees destruction, destruction of what had once been a jet. He shakes it off, and the jet returns to normal again.
With a determined expression, he pushes the record button again.

Not that I'm worried, because I trust them, you know? Kirsten did tell me to take a nap, after all, and she wouldn't say that unless she knew I could, would she? I know that they'll make it out okay, drink their beer and just forget about me. I have to, you know? Thinking that anything else happened would just…destroy me. I don't think I could go on then.
I think I heard a sound…I'm not sure though. I'm not as fully trained as they are, getaway-jet, remember? But still.
<em>Long pause.
</em>
Nah, must've been my imagination. I imagine a lot, you know? Like…the last man I had to question for them. It was almost…as if he was planning my death, you know? But Rake told me that I wasn't important, and that they don't go after unimportant people. And I can't be important, can I? Rake would've noticed me if I was, wouldn't he? He would've taken me out by now, right? But I'm not important, now am I? I wonder if he and Kirsten went out already…the rumour was that they were planning to. But I can't stop them, can I? Or else I would-
<em>Another long pause.
</em>
Now I really think I heard something. Rake would've checked it out by now, you know? But I'm not Rake, now am I? But still…I wonder what's out there, you know? I was complaining about how I was stuck here, but now it never seemed so clear to me just how stuck I am, you know? If they…if <em>they </em>would be out there, I would have no where to go, you know? But they aren't out there, are they? What would they want with such an unimportant one as me? Still I wonder, you know? I really think I heard something…
And I don't even know what's going on there, you know? I'm still in the dark, not on top of things…Just because I wear glasses, doesn't mean I'm noisy, or that I know <em>everything...</em> But I wish I did, you know? I wish I did know something.
Like what's out there…
Rake would have my ass for sitting here, fussing like a baby, all the while wasting valuable batteries on my whacked out hallucinations. Then again, Rake would rather have me spend hundreds of dollars on a shrink than a few cents on batteries. I suppose he <em>is</em> a bit of a nutcase… I suppose we <em>all</em> are a bit of a nutcase. And not sleeping for three days straight doesn't help either, now, does it…? Yeah. I am a bit of a nutcase, aren't I? It's good to know these things about yourself. It's good to –
<em>Thud.</em>
<em>Long pause.</em>
Did… Did you… I <em>heard</em>…

He pushes stop, and rewinds a bit. The thud is repeated. It's <em>there</em>. Something is <em>there</em>. Or someone… Is this it? His first warning, the first sign that something is wrong? He closes his eyes, fighting back the tears.
"Did… Did you… I <em>heard</em>…" the tape repeats. Then there is a soft clicking sound, then nothing. The tape is left to run freely, no previous recording on it. In his mind, it's not his voice speaking. It's hers. Did she know? Did she know what was coming to her?
He shakes his head, to get rid of the images flowing into his mind. Then he presses the record button again.

I can't sleep. I can't <em>possibly</em> sleep. I can't stop talking into this tape… If I stop, I see… I see… I don't want to see anything, not yet. I'm not ready. I'm not ready, damn it! Just like she wasn't – No. I can't go there, I can't! I… They are taking a <em>long</em> time, aren't they? I can't imagine why they wouldn't call. They should call. Now! I'm sure they're laughing now… It's probably a practical joke, those morons. Those insensitive bastards. Those –

He stops the tape, and rewinds it just a bit, to take out some particularly vivid swearing.

I bet they're right outside, occasionally banging a tube on one of the wings or whatever… Waiting to see me running out, scared as hell, pants wet… Well, I'm not coming! They can sort out their own problems… No, that's not true. If they have <em>problems</em>, I'll come help them, obviously. I'm the getaway-jet, it'd be pretty much beside the point if I got away without them… But I am <em>not </em>coming out of this jet. In fact, I'm going to stay <em>right</em> here.
I'm <em>not </em>going out there to face it, like she did. I'm not going to face what's coming for me. I'm not running back to get a weapon only to have the jet explode on me. I'm staying in this jet, waiting for the joke to end. Because they're joking, right?
I can't stop shaking, did she shake? I can't stop talking either…I wonder how she is doing now. I wonder if Kirsten misses her. I wonder a lot of things as long as I don't have to wonder what they are going to do to me. Whoever they are…I wonder who they are. If…if this isn't a practical joke, of course, because if it is, I already know who they are and what they are going to do to me. Probably laugh, mock, and then laugh some more. Especially Rake, he would really think this is a-
<em>Thud.</em>
