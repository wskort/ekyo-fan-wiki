# Mrs. Ferrars
She is the mother of [[Fanny Dashwood]], [[Edward Ferrars]], and [[Robert Ferrars]]. She is the mother-in-law of [[John Dashwood]]. 

She is a bad-tempered, unsympathetic woman who shares all the foibles of [[Fanny Dashwood|Fanny]] and [[Robert Ferrars|Robert]]. She is determined that her sons should marry well, even though the late Mr. Ferrars left them a fortune.
