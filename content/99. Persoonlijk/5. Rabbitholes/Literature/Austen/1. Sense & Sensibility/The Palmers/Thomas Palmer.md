# Thomas Palmer
[[Thomas Palmer]] is a rather serious man married to a rather [[Charlotte Palmer|silly young woman]], and he's never quite recovered from this fact. We don't know much about [[Thomas Palmer|Mr. Palmer]], but what we do know paints a somewhat comical, contradictory picture. First of all, our first impressions of him are of a rude, sardonic, disinterested man, who seems to despise everyone else under the sun. However, as we get to know him a little better, we start to warm up to him.
