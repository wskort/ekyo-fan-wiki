# Mrs. Dashwood
Mother of [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]], [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] and [[Margaret Dashwood|Margaret]]. As she was the second wife, and the first wife had begotten a [[John Dashwood|son]], she is left penniless when [[Henry Dashwood|her husband]] passes away.
