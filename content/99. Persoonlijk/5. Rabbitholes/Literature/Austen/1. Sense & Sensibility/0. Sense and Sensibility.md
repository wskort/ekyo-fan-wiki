# Sense and Sensibility
When [[Henry Dashwood|Mr. Henry Dashwood]] dies, leaving all his money to his first wife's son [[John Dashwood]], his [[Mrs. Dashwood|second wife]] and her three daughters are left with no permanent home and very little income. [[Mrs. Dashwood]] and her daughters ([[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]], [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]], and [[Margaret Dashwood|Margaret]]) are invited to stay with their distant relations, the [[Lady Middleton|Middletons]], at Barton Park. [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] is sad to leave their home at Norland because she has become closely attached to [[Edward Ferrars]], the brother-in-law of her half-brother [[John Dashwood|John]]. However, once at Barton Park, [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] and [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] discover many new acquaintances, including the retired officer and bachelor [[Colonel Brandon]], and the gallant and impetuous [[John Willoughby]], who rescues [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] after she twists her ankle running down the hills of Barton in the rain. [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]] openly and unabashedly courts [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]], and together the two flaunt their attachment to one another, until [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]] suddenly announces that he must depart for London on business, leaving [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] lovesick and miserable. Meanwhile, [[Anne Steele|Anne]] and [[Lucy Steele]], two recently discovered relations of [[Lady Middleton]]'s mother, [[Mrs. Jennings]], arrive at Barton Park as guests of the [[Lady Middleton|Middletons]]. [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]] ingratiates herself to [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] and informs her that she ([[Lucy Steele|Lucy]]) has been secretly engaged to [[Edward Ferrars|Mr. Ferrars]] for a whole year. [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] initially assumes that [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]] is referring to [[Edward Ferrars|Edward]]'s younger brother, [[Robert Ferrars|Robert]], but is shocked and pained to learn that [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]] is actually referring to her own beloved [[Edward Ferrars|Edward]].

In Volume II of the novel, [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] and [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] travel to London with [[Mrs. Jennings]]. [[Colonel Brandon]] informs [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] that everyone in London is talking of an engagement between [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]] and [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]], though [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] has not told her family of any such attachment. [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] is anxious to be reunited with her beloved [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]], but when she sees him at a party in town, he cruelly rebuffs her and then sends her a letter denying that he ever had feelings for her. [[Colonel Brandon]] tells [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] of [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]]'s history of callousness and debauchery, and [[Mrs. Jennings]] confirms that [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]], having squandered his fortune, has become engaged to the wealthy heiress [[Sophia Grey]].

In Volume III, [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]]'s [[Anne Steele|older sister]] inadvertently reveals the news of [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]]'s secret engagement to [[Edward Ferrars]]. [[Edward Ferrars|Edward]]'s mother is outraged at the information and disinherits him, promising his fortune to [[Robert Ferrars|Robert]] instead. Meanwhile, the [[Elinor Dashwood|Dash]][[Marianne Dashwood|wood]] sisters visit family friends at Cleveland on their way home from London. At Cleveland, [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] develops a severe cold while taking long walks in the rain, and she falls deathly ill. Upon hearing of her illness, [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]] comes to visit, attempting to explain his misconduct and seek forgiveness. [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]] pities him and ultimately shares his story with [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]], who finally realizes that she behaved imprudently with [[John Willoughby|Willoughby]] and could never have been happy with him anyway. [[Mrs. Dashwood]] and [[Colonel Brandon]] arrive at Cleveland and are relieved to learn that [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] has begun to recover.

When the [[Elinor Dashwood|Dash]][[Marianne Dashwood|woods]] return to Barton, they learn from their manservant that [[Lucy Steele]] and [[Robert Ferrars|Mr. Ferrars]] are engaged. They assume that he means [[Edward Ferrars]], and are thus unsurprised, but [[Edward Ferrars|Edward]] himself soon arrives and corrects their misconception: it was [[Robert Ferrars|Robert]], not himself, whom the money-grubbing [[Lucy Steele|Lucy]] ultimately decided to marry. Thus, [[Edward Ferrars|Edward]] is finally free to propose to his beloved [[Elinor Dashwood|Elinor]], and not long after, [[Marianne Dashwood|Marianne]] and [[Colonel Brandon]] become engaged as well. The couples live together at Delaford and remain in close touch with their mother and [[Margaret Dashwood|younger sister]] at Barton Cottage.
