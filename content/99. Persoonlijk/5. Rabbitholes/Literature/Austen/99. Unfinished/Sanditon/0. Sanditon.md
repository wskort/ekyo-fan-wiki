# Sanditon
The story begins when Tom and [[Mary Parker]]'s carriage crashes on a hill near Willingden, [[Mr. Heywood]]'s estate, and [[Tom Parker|Mr. Parker]]'s ankle is sprained, so he and [[Mary Parker|his wife]] stay with the Heywood family for a fortnight. During their stay, [[Tom Parker|Mr. Parker]] tells the Heywoods that he and his business partner, [[Lady Denham]], are planning on making Sanditon, a small town, into a fashionable seaside resort. After [[Tom Parker|Mr. Parker]]'s ankle is healed, he and [[Mary Parker|Mrs. Parker]] invite [[Charlotte Heywood]], the oldest daughter at home, to join them on a visit.

When she arrives at Sanditon, [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] meets some of the residents, including [[Lady Denham]], Miss Brereton, [[Sir Edward Denham]] and [[Esther Denham|Miss Denham]]Miss Denham. [[Lady Denham]] is described as the lady of the town ('every neighbourhood should have a great Lady'), and upon talking to her [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] finds her 'thoroughly mean'. [[Clara Brereton|Miss Clara Brereton]] is [[Lady Denham]]'s poor relation and was taken in to stay at Sanditon House. She is described as 'elegantly tall, regularly handsome, with a great delicacy of complexion and soft blue eyes, with a sweetly modest and yet naturally graceful Address'. [[Sir Edward Denham]] and [[Esther Denham|Miss Esther Denham]] are sister and brother, and [[Lady Denham]] believes they are both out to get her money. [[Sir Edward Denham|Sir Edward]] is silly and fairly handsome, and [[Esther Denham|Miss Denham]] is aloof and cold.

Visitors come to Trafalgar House (where [[Tom Parker|Tom]] and [[Mary Parker]] live): they are [[Diana Parker]], [[Arthur Parker]], and [[Susan Parker]]. In a letter [[Tom Parker|Tom]] received earlier from [[Diana Parker|Diana]], they were described to have very awful sicknesses, but upon closer observation [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] finds that they are in well enough health, and she concludes that they must like to exaggerate. [[Diana Parker|Diana]]'s goal is to bring a family from the West Indies to Sanditon, as well as a girl's school. When [[Mrs. Griffiths]] arrives, they find that the two families are the same. Accompanying [[Mrs. Griffiths]] is [[Miss Lambe]] and two Miss Beauforts. [[Lady Denham]] wants [[Sir Edward Denham|Sir Edward]] to marry [[Miss Lambe]], who is a wealthy heiress.

When walking with [[Mary Parker|Mary]] and her daughter ([[Little Mary Parker|Little Mary]]), [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] meets [[Sidney Parker]], who is heading to a hotel. [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] sees him as 'seven or eight and twenty, very good looking, with a decided air of Ease and Fashion, and a lively countenance'. They continue without him, and head to Sanditon House, where [[Lady Denham]] lives. [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] sees [[Clara Brereton|Miss Brereton]] and [[Sir Edward Denham|Sir Edward]] sitting very close together and talking gently. [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] is surprised by [[Clara Brereton|Miss Brereton]], but leaves them to their privacy, and doesn't tell [[Mary Parker|Mary]]. The story ends when they enter the house, and [[Charlotte Heywood|Charlotte]] notices a large portrait of [[Sir Harry Denham]] and a small portrait of [[Lady Denham]]'s first husband.
