# The Watsons
[[Emma Watson]] was raised by her wealthy aunt and uncle in the expectation that she would be their heir, but after her uncle dies, her aunt remarries and she is sent to live with her father and sisters, whom she has not seen in fourteen years. [[Mr. Watson|Her father]] is sick, and when he dies, [[Emma Watson|Emma]] and her sisters will be penniless. They are therefore desperate to be married: [[Elizabeth Watson|Elizabeth]], the eldest sister, mourns her first love [[Purvis]] but will marry any man who is not disagreeable; [[Penelope Watson|Penelope]] has gone to Chichester to pursue the elderly and asthmatic [[Dr. Harding]], and [[Margaret Watson|Margaret]] has gone to Croydon in another bid to make the wealthy ne'er-do-well [[Tom Musgrave]] miss her.

[[Emma Watson|Emma]] is invited to attend the first winter assembly with [[Mr. Edwards|Mr.]] and [[Mrs. Edwards]], whose daughter [[Mary Edwards|Mary]] is an heiress pursued by [[Emma Watson|Emma]]'s brother [[Sam Watson|Sam]] as well as [[Captain Hunter]]. [[Emma Watson|Emma]] dances with ten-year-old [[Charles Blake]], which brings her to the attention of [[Lord Osborne]], [[Charles Blake|Charles]]'s uncle [[Mr. Howard]], who was [[Lord Osborne]]'s tutor and is now the clergyman of the parish, and the women of their party: [[Lord Osborne]]'s mother [[Lady Osborne]], his sister [[Miss Osborne]] and her friend [[Fanny Carr]], and [[Charles Blake|Charles]]'s mother [[Mrs. Blake]], a widow who keeps house for [[Mr. Howard|her brother]]. [[Emma Watson|Emma]] dances with [[Mr. Howard]] and comes to admire him.

The next morning [[Emma Watson|Emma]] is stranded at the [[Mr. Edwards|Edw]][[Mrs. Edwards|ard]][[Mary Edwards|ses]]' when [[Mr. Watson|her father]] takes the chair to attend the bishop's visitation, and she is offered a ride in his curricle by [[Tom Musgrave|Musgrave]]; she refuses and is instead brought home by the [[Mr. Edwards|Edw]][[Mrs. Edwards|ard]][[Mary Edwards|ses]]' carriage. Days later [[Tom Musgrave|Musgrave]] visits with [[Lord Osborne]], who shows [[Emma Watson|Emma]] marked attention and invites her to see him off on his next hunt; she does not go, and because [[Mr. Watson|their father]] cannot return the visit, they dispatch [[Tom Musgrave|Musgrave]] with an apology to [[Lord Osborne]].

[[Margaret Watson|Margaret]] returns after a month in Croydon with their brother [[Robert Watson|Robert]] and his wife [[Jane Watson|Jane]], and on the same evening, [[Tom Musgrave|Musgrave]] happens to visit on his way back from London. He plays cards with them and repeats gossip from Osborne Castle, but he is unable to tell [[Emma Watson|Emma]] about a conversation [[Mr. Howard]] and [[Lord Osborne]] had regarding her before he is interrupted. He is invited back to dinner the next day but does not come, which puts [[Margaret Watson|Margaret]] in a bad humor. [[Robert Watson|Robert]] and [[Jane Watson|Jane]] leave to return home after inviting [[Emma Watson|Emma]] to join them in Croydon; she refuses. The draft ends there.

According to [[Jane Austen#Cassandra Austen|Cassandra Austen]], [[Mr. Watson]] would soon die, and his four unmarried daughters would have to depend upon [[Robert Watson|Robert]] and [[Jane Watson|Jane]] for a home. [[Emma Watson|Emma]] would reject a proposal from [[Lord Osborne]], and after tribulations regarding [[Lady Osborne]]'s passion for [[Mr. Howard]] and his for [[Emma Watson|Emma]], [[Emma Watson|Emma]] and [[Mr. Howard]] would marry.
