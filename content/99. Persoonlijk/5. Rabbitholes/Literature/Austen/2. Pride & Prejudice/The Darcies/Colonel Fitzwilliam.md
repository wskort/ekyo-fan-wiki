# Colonel Fitzwilliam
Nephew of [[Lady Catherine de Bourgh]] and cousin of [[Fitzwilliam Darcy]] and [[Georgiana Darcy]]. He and [[Fitzwilliam Darcy|Mr. Darcy]] are the guardians of [[Georgiana Darcy]]. 

While he flirts a bit with [[Elizabeth Watson|Elizabeth]], he warns her that she must marry for money in order to support himself, meaning they can never be more than friends.
