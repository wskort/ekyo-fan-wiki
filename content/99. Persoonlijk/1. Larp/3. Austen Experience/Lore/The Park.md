## The Park
The Park is a large estate in the countryside, an old house that has been restored to its former glory. The grounds are owned by the Company and run by an event division that cooperates with a scientific division. The Park itself is run by a supervisor, Mal, who has seen to the renovations and improvements to the estate. They are in charge of a small team of people who work with practical things at the estate – props, food and more. It has been running for a couple of years but has now taken a step to become a more commercial experience. 
### The Park in the Fiction
In the fiction of the Park, the estate is known as [[The Park|Longbourn Park]]. It is owned by Lady Mal de Courcy, and is staffed by the Hill and Smith families. The neighbouring village is home to several families, many of them with the surname Price (the Company employees). The Heroes and Heroines at [[The Park|Longbourn Park]] have been invited to come and stay with Lady de Courcy for her annual ball, either because she knows their families or because she has heard of them somehow. The ball is a big social event that all unmarried young people hope to score an invitation to. The Heroes and Heroines are the people who were selected this year. Lady de Courcy’s balls are known for ending with the announcement of several engagements, so this is the expectation that the Park characters have, going into the experience.
