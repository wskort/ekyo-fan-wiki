# AI units
The Artificial Intelligence research was kickstarted when the Company had to quickly adapt to the massive hole in the service market left behind by the Android recall. The simplistic and very limited AI:s that had been developed before were quickly discarded. In their place, a top secret project was launched with the goal of creating AI:s that could be made to control and exist within a virtual or physical body. 

Two years ago, the Moon project was finished, delivering the first AI to market that was indistinguishable from a human when people chatted with it, and that had the potential for controlling its own appearance inside a virtual space. Now, the time has come to use similar (but less powerful) AI configurations inside physical bodies – a biological Android body, created without a brain, with a machine in its stead.

The AI units used at the Austen Experience are based on machine-learning, in that they develop on their own once they are out among people. They collect data, they learn and their personalities change according to their initial parameters and motivations. This is of course highly limited and secure, but it still means that their programmers don’t always know exactly how or why they behave the way they do. Each AI unit does have a set of personality traits that govern their persona, both in the Park and outside it, that they will not stray from. These are their parameters. 
  
The AI units follow the laws of robotics, meaning that they will not harm a human being, or take an action that will lead to the harm of a human being, and that they must follow the orders given to them by a human – here defined as a Company employee.

The AI units have three different modes:
### Park Mode
In order for the Park to function, the AI units have Park Personas. They are the same sort of character that everyone else receives, with the big exception that the AI units are not aware of any other existence while they’re in their Park mode. They will never reference something outside the fiction, they will not remember anything that happened outside the fiction, and they will shy away from any such talk that others are prone to do. The Park Personas are programmed in a more simple fashion than the core of the AI units, and as such, they will simply ignore things that appear out of the fiction, such as people talking about the Company, or “plotlines” or things that don’t fit into the Regency reality. If prompted, a Park Persona will say that they don’t understand what someone is talking about if people try to discuss such things with them.   

The Park Personas are reset every evening, and their storyline starts from scratch in the mornings. This could potentially be made exceptions to, if the Company decides to, but that has not been extensively tested. 

An AI unit can be set into Park mode by a Company employee, as well as be taken out of it.
### Compiler Mode
In Compiler mode, the AI units are at their fullest cognitive potential. They remember the things that happened to them in the Park fiction, but they also know that they are AI:s and can discuss this on a theoretical level. However, they still maintain the same personality and the same machine-developed learning that they’ve gathered since their last **full reset**. Some of them have gone a long time since they were fully reset back to factory defaults or some backup, and as such, they might have developed in ways that the programmer didn’t predict. This is not some sort of full sentience – just the AI unit learning to act in new ways, still according to their rules and parameters. 

Full resets take them back to their last backup or to their original factory default. Company employees can back them up or reset them completely. 

Hanging out with an AI unit in Compiler mode is a bit like spending time with a co-worker that knows it is an artificial intelligence without real emotions. It will still act like it has them, but won’t break any rules. 
### Passive Mode
In Passive mode, the AI unit loses all personality and behaves like a very simple robot. It still has all its memories, but will only tell them if asked directly. It shows no emotion unless prompted, it does not take any initiatives and only responds to direct commands. Commands can be anything that the Company employee can think up, within reason. 

This mode is used to try to fix bugs, correct behavior or just to change their personality, storylines or other parameters that the AI units have. 
## Parameters
The AI unit personalities have six parameters. These rule their behavior both inside and outside the Park, but with a focus on their Park persona. These parameters can be changed by Company employees with simple voice commands (this will be explained to the Company staff separately), but each unit starts the larp with a set number. The parameters are:

**Competition**: How competitive the persona is. This could manifest through wagers, challenges, jealousy or in other manners. Someone with low competition might step aside for someone who is more of a bully, or might back down from challenges.
**Romance**: Their romantic tendencies. This could be an interest in poetry or how courteous they are, how highly they value a romantic connection vs a financial or status-based one, and how often they engage in romantic gestures.
Intelligence: Not intended as pure computing power, but rather how well educated they are, how intellectual or analytical the person is. Someone with high intelligence is more likely to engage in deep conversation while someone with low intelligence would rather engage in activities.
**Deceit**: How willing they are to lie, cheat and deceive their way to their goals. They might cheat on their partners, or manipulate and trick people into believing stories that aren’t true. They might also refuse to tell someone about their background or previous mistakes – or vice versa, someone with low deceit might feel compelled to tell the truth even when it harms them.
**Wit**: How funny, joking or generally charming someone is in conversation and manners. Low wit might manifest in not quite carrying conversations, getting lost in the social game or failing to entertain. 
**Selfishness**: How centered their world view is on themselves. This is not the same as deceit – a carefree playboy might have high selfishness but low deceit. They don’t intend to harm, they just do it through naivéty or their inability to see the perspectives of others. Low selfishness might mean that they are martyrs, sacrificing themselves, or that they are simply not good at looking to their own needs.

**Full Reset** is not the same as an AIs storyline being reset (Which is done every morning, usually). It is a very drastic measure that resets the AI unit to its factory defaults, losing a lot of learned behaviours and reverting to its basic model. This is done in case an AI unit is starting to diverge too much from its intended function and cannot be fixed through the normal means, but the Company is reluctant to do it due to the loss of a lot of valuable learned behaviours and time required to relearn them.
