# Androids
The following is some basic information about the Androids, the creations that defined everyone’s lives up until six years ago. Nowadays, only the old Android models from more than a decade back are being produced – simple things that only work mechanical jobs for mass-scale industries, though that was not the case some time ago. If you are familiar with the Androids Universe, and its fiction about Androids, you do not have to read the following, but if you are unfamiliar, here is a short summary:
- Androids were, and are, not machines in any way. They are flesh and bone, essentially identical to humans, both on the inside and out, with a few important differences:
- Androids are grown in vats and created by “programming” DNA and designing their organs, muscles and such with certain functions in mind. 
- Their brains in particular are created separately and with specific neurological modifications to incline them to different behaviours or with certain skills. They are called Androids in-game because the humans of the world wanted them to feel alien, more akin to machines, in order for the populace to accept the conditions they existed in, and the roles they were built for. Their pre-existing memories are designed and planted in order to give them the right kind of associations.
- Each Android is created with a modification to their genes that will cause a rapidly spreading cell degeneration after a certain amount of time, hyper-rapidly aging organs inside them, causing death over the span of a few hours. The allotted life-span for an Android is usually 4 years. They cannot have children.
- The fixed 4-year limit was decided upon once early experiments with Androids demonstrated that they became unpredictable and that their neurological programming started to change in significant ways as time passed. Their bodies and brains adapted and developed new ways to function, and it was determined to be ethical and practical to have the Androids expire before that happened.
## Models
There are different Android series. The last ones were the N6s, the ones that were determined unsafe six years ago and retired from the market. What is known is that all of them were killed in a giant purge – the Recall. The older models were kept in production and use. 

The ones at the Austen Experience are the N5 models, the fifth series of Androids. They are genetically programmed, designed to work with a specific task or with a specific mindset. The ones at the Austen Experience are competent at appearing human within the scopes of their roles or their functions as Austen Heroes, but not much further. Their emotional depth is mainly on the surface and just deep enough to play out their parts, but not encouraging any independent, new thoughts or emotional reactions. They lack any strong sense of empathy and understanding for others, but are intelligent – albeit in a limited way. 

The Androids at the Park are one of the following four models:
### Historical Reenactment Model
This model is made to feel comfortable in a certain time period, which in this case is mostly the Regency period. They are created with historical memories and feel a bit out of place in the modern world. In general, these models feel more comfortable as their Park character than they do out of it. Originally their purpose was to star in period dramas or be part of vast museums, but some of them have instead been put to work at the Park, with mixed results. There are many variations of the model, so they differ a lot from each other.
### Romantic Companion Model
This model was made to be a romantic partner to a human. They are friendly, helpful and want to please and worship their mate. They care deeply for the Heroines they’re matched up with, and are heartbroken when the Heroines leave the Park. Even though they have now gone through this several times, they are still affected by their emotions. They hope that one day they will find true love with a guest.
### Best Friend Model
This model is born with memories of a close friend who passed away, and they spend their time searching for another friendship to replace it. They can be romantic, but amorous love is second to companionship for them. They use romance to find someone that understands them, and will treat them like a best friend. They usually aren’t passionate lovers, but loyal and loving companions in their relationships.
### Service Model
This model was made to work in customer service or any kind of service job. They were created with memories of being congratulated on doing a good job, and of getting through hard times at work. They want to do a good job, in the Park and outside of it, but they don’t necessarily have the creative skills to navigate the Austen fiction as well as some of the other models. Still, they will always give it their all, hoping to make their customers happy.
