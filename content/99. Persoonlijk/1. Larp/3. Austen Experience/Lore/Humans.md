# Humans
During the [[Androids|Android]] era, Humans didn’t need to work. Everyone had basic income, and the economy moved from a monetary one to a Fame based one. You needed to be visible and influential to hold power in that world. Those who didn’t find a way to be famous faded into a bleak existence, sometimes wishing for their old jobs back, or becoming a part of the anti-Android movement.

After the Android Recall, when all [[Androids|N6 Models]] were rounded up and killed, they left a gap in the workforce, and regular people needed to go back to work. Money became the financial fuel of society again. The biggest employer is the [[0. Company|Company]], but several more businesses have opened up these days, to take over small portions of the market.
