## To [[Varuna|Miss Varuna]]

My dear [[Varuna|Miss Taylor]], 

Oh what sweet perfume my salty air has pierced!
My weary heart has never been so warmed
So briefly did we meet, yet I now yearn to call you "dear'st,"
Although you had so wisely shielded me and made me warned
Please be kind and do not leave me scorned

[[Captain Thomas Graham]]
