## [[Skyler|Miss Skyler Wright]]

Dear [[Skyler|Miss Skyler]],

I watch love blossom, always from afar
At times, I help it bloom
To mine own heart, the door was left ajar
Your ***hope*** has spelled my doom
For I have struggled fiercely, yet in vain
It simply will not do
You must allow me to reveal it plain
How much I yearn for you
I know not when my love for you began
Or why your eyes can fill me so with glee
For what comes next, I do not have a plan
But one ill word from you shall silence me

[[River|Smith]]
