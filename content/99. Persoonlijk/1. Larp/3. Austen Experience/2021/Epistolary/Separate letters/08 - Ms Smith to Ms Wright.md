## To [[Skyler|Ms Wright]]

Spinning frocks across the hall
Glances stolen from beyond
Not a soul that knows our bond
Stealing practice for the ball
May the music never fade
May our dance last through the days
Hearts a-flutter in our stays
Be my lady,
			Love,
				[[River|Your maid]]
