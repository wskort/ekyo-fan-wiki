# To [[Skyler|Ms Skyler]]

My dearest [[Skyler]],

As equals you would have us
In beauty, grace and flair
If any would behold us
They'd say we're quite the pair
So boldly I have kissed you
Your broken heart to win
This treasure I shall give you
And I shall wear its twin

Your [[River]]
