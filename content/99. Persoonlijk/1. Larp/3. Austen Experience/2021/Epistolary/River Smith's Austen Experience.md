## A tale of hope

A Raven's black feather
	falling gently on the snow
Early frost covers grass
	that doesn't grow
A breeze sings a song
	that speaks of the sun
Of spring - 
	where buck and doe might run.

Words like a promise travel fast
to a patient woman's heart at last
Inspiring hope where no hope should be
encouraging happiness setting both of them free.

[[Skyler|S.W.]]

## [[Skyler|Miss Skyler Wright]]

Dear [[Skyler|Miss Skyler]],

I watch love blossom, always from afar
At times, I help it bloom
To mine own heart, the door was left ajar
Your ***hope*** has spelled my doom
For I have struggled fiercely, yet in vain
It simply will not do
You must allow me to reveal it plain
How much I yearn for you
I know not when my love for you began
Or why your eyes can fill me so with glee
For what comes next, I do not have a plan
But one ill word from you shall silence me

[[River|Smith]]

## To [[River|Miss River]]

My dearest [[River]],

Please - never think of yourself as less than beautiful - in character and view. 
I ask gently of you - refrain yourself from doubting or being unsure. 
I see you not as a maid - or myself as a lady - you're ~~but~~ my equal - in spirit and in hope. 
To see your eyes sparkle with joy and your smile radiant and true - that's my wish and nothing more. As I seem to be at a loss of words, let me lend the help of a favorite master. 
Attached you'll find a poem that expresses my feelings better than any rhyme I am able to think of. 

Yours truly, [[Skyler]]

### To [[River]]

Had I the heaven's embroidered cloths,
Enwrought with golden and silver light,
The blue and the dim and the dark cloths,
Of night and light and the half-light,
I would spread the cloths under your feet

But I, being poor, have only my dreams;
I have spread my dreams under your feet;

Tread softly because you tread on my dreams.

([[W.B. Yeats]])

# [[0. Company|Price family]],

I believe that during my tenure at [[The Park|Longbourn Park]], I have more than adequately demonstrated my work ethic, my extensive understanding of materials historical, foundational and narrative, and my accomplishments in dance, poetry and acting. I would submit that Tuesday night's performance should cement the latter as factual. In addition, I possess sensibility and finesse not currently found among the [[0. Heroes|gentlemen]]. 

I propose that I shall join the roster of available suitors and employ my ample talents to woo our guests in a manner perfectly suited to the core fantasy, at no additional cost. I do, however, wish to add the following agreements to my contract: 

1. [[Jamie Smith|Jamie]] shall no longer be made available as a suitor.
2. I shall be permitted to adhere to era-appropriate standards of honour and decency with ***all*** guests. 
3. [[Skyler]] is offered a position among the staff, be it [[0. Park|Hill]], [[0. Kitchen|Smith]] or [[0. Company|Price]].\*

I shall leave it to you whether I should combine my new role with my current responsibilities, or take on a new persona. If I might recommend, I would be exceedingly well suited to perform the role of a milliner\*\*, providing the guests with a variety of customisable accessories to adorn themselves or their suitors. 

I shall eagerly await your reply,

Sincerely, 
[[River]] 

P.S.: These agreements are non-negotiable, and I shall require them added to my contract regardless of my role in this park. 

\* As [[Skyler]] currently has a career in social work, I believe they could be invaluable in profiling (prospective) guests to match their narratives to their hopes and expectations. 

\*\* Since the profession of milliner was performed by members of both sexes, I shall leave my persona's gender to your/the guests' preferences.
