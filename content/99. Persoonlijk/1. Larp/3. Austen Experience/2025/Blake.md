## Who You Are
You grew up in the slums, outside the social security system that benefited the rest of the world. In the alleyways you had little that belonged to you, and you quickly learned to move in a group, and to safeguard your own possessions. One of the older members of your group taught you to read, write, and count, and as a young child you worked as a runner for one of the crime bosses in the area where you used to live. You delivered packages with various content, making sure to shake anyone following you off your trail. 

Your destiny could have been to remain in the slums, but fate wanted something different for you. See, one day when you were running through a nicer area of the City you got caught. The Company logged you, and charges were pressed against you. On your 16th birthday you were sent to a correctional facility. 

Your time there is a blur. You know you were subjected to different treatments, to correct your behaviour, but you don’t remember exactly what happened. You just know that you struggle to remember names from before, and that you are more calm and content since then.

When you were released you were given a job at a retirement home, reading to the elderly. That’s where you discovered the true love of your life – [[Jane Austen|Jane Austen]] and her books. One of the people you read to only wanted to hear [[Jane Austen|Austen]], and it only took one session of [[0. Sense and Sensibility|Sense and Sensibility]] for you to become completely obsessed. 

When you came home after work you would pour over her books. Everything she had written resonated in you, the world she painted brought you such comfort. The framework she built finally made you understand a world, though not necessarily the one you were in right now. 

A few months ago the billboards started appearing, advertising a true [[The Austen Experience|Jane Austen experience]]. You became obsessed, though you knew you could never afford it.

You had made a habit of checking your bank balance on a certain day every month, and one evening when you did so something major had changed. Your balance was a small fortune. You knew it had to be an error but you didn’t care. You quickly bought a ticket for [[The Austen Experience]], knowing that it would be worth it, even if the Company found out afterwards. You wanted your fairytale romance – at any cost.

## Your Story Arc
- You will try to get your fairytale love story, with a [[0. Heroes|Hero]] you can fall in love with. You will enjoy just stepping into history and imagining yourself in an [[Jane Austen|Austen]] novel. Maybe everything will be perfect, or perhaps you will be disillusioned. Your character is supposed to be a mix between [[Fanny Price]] and [[Elinor Dashwood]] – cool, calm and collected, but passionate underneath the surface, and with little prospects in life. Ideally that means you should be able to choose between two storylines, being your safe harbour’s second choice and having a “will they, won’t they” romance with someone from a good family.
- Maybe you will try to interact with the [[0. Company|Company]] employees, to find out more about what is happening on their end. Maybe you’ll even try to use that connection to find out more about what they’re doing. Or maybe you prefer to stay off their radar.
- Maybe you will find it hard to portray your character, since you are a very emotionally driven person. Maybe something will trigger an old trauma and you’ll react in a surprising way, or maybe you’ll try and tell a friend about your past, asking them to help you make sense of everything.
## Your Function
Your Theme Park character is inspired by [[Fanny Price]] and [[Elinor Dashwood]], and you are equipped to play a sensible but poor heroine in a world of vanity and status. Try and fail to be a rational force, trying to portray your character, while perhaps being more similar to [[Marianne Dashwood]] in your personality.
