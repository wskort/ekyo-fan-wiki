# Miss Blake Earnest
**Group:** [[0. Bronze|Bronze level]] [[0. Heroines|Heroine]]
**Income:** Orphan of no fortune
[[Jane Austen|Austen]] quote:
*“There will be some difficulty in our way, [[Mrs. Norris]]… as to the distinction proper to be made between the girls as they grow up… how, without depressing her spirits too far, to make her remember that [[Fanny Price|she]] is not a [[Maria Bertram|Miss]] [[Julia Bertram|Bertram]]… they cannot be equals. Their rank, fortune, rights, and expectations will always be different.”*

## Tragic Backstory
You were orphaned by your poor mother, who left you and your brother when she couldn’t support you any longer. For a long time, you had no home and no outlook on life at all, but that changed when the [[Andie|Springs]] family took you in. Your brother was taken in by another family, but you still exchange letters with him. You miss him terribly. 
## Description
You had to learn to be righteous, strong and to stand on your own two feet. You could never be allowed a slip in character or judgment, and you were always the one taking responsibility for things – even things that someone else ought to have handled or that should have been done already. Some of your friends call you insufferable, or a bore, or you suspect they just find you annoying. So be it. Someone has to be the person that others depend on, that takes things seriously and that doesn’t squander the life they have been given. You came from nothing, had nothing and now you have carved a life out for yourself. 

You owe the [[Andie|Springs]] family as much. But, no woman’s life is complete without a man, and you hope, you are convinced, that your character and integrity will be just right for a proper man out there, somewhere.

You would probably turn down most men, because if your feelings and motivations are not pure, you wouldn’t subject a man to a marriage that does not match the expectations he holds. 
## Deep, Dark Secret
You were never treated well by the family who took you in. [[Andie|Duchess Andie Springs]] could have been like a sister to you but instead resents you. You know you should be grateful, but you have a lot of emotional wounds from the whole family’s treatment of you. Most of it was just small, nagging reminders that you were lesser, that you were not truly a part of the family and that you couldn’t expect much of your life. You would never speak about this, and your guilt over feeling ungrateful or critical of them is often overpowering.

## Plot
You are in the park to be an example for many of the adventurous or passionate, wild young women – and men, for that matter. You are a great support for those in emotional need, but you tend to put your own needs last. You judge people a great deal, berate them for their choices and act a bit holier-than-thou against the other lady guests who aren’t living up to standards. 

Your lonely and distanced disposition might of course change when you meet the right, upstanding man, who will find his way into your heart and ease your troubles. He is out there, you just have to be certain of his affections and of yours.

Someone like [[Sir Robert Milton]] could be appropriate, but [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Thomas Hales]] has also expressed an interest in you. You’ve met him before, and you feel a little flattered over the attention - against your own better judgment.
