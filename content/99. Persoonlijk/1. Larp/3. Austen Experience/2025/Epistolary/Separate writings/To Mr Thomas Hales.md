### To [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales]]

I have read many stories of impoverished young women, kind of heart and gentle of soul, unaware of the dangers outside their doors. I read aloud of their trials and tribulations, the cruelty of the world and of wolves in sheep’s clothing. I deliver the comforting lines of the heroic gentlemen that save them from the brink of ruin when all hope seems lost. There is solace in Fiction: the Good end up happy and the Bad end unhappily. 

But orphans such as ourselves know not to rely on kindness. We learn to move in a pack, and to guard what is ours. Some learn to hunt, others learn to evade. 

A discarded ember left in the streets burned bright as day, consuming anything that careless hands abandoned. Crushed underfoot, its flames were killed and scattered. Doused in water, kept in a jar, years went by in icy torpor. I came here, risking the loss of what little agency is left to me, wishing for a humble spark. You, sir, are ablaze. 

[[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]], I write to you because I overheard [[Varuna|Miss Varuna]] brag of her plans to trick you, to lie to you, and tell you she’d never consider letting you anywhere near her. I do not mean to stop either of you. I wish you a happy hunt, and I am eager to see if you can bloody the water before it is time for tea. 

You’ll need to hurry if you plan to pick [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|a thornier rose]].
