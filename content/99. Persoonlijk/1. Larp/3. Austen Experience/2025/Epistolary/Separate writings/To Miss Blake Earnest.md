### To [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Miss Blake Earnest]]

First of all, allow me to reassure you that this is no trick, not part of a game nor a jest at your expense.

You have done something I have never seen a woman achieve before with such calm indifference, and it brings me pause. You have left me questioning myself, and my propensity to pursue love in its frivolous and fleeting form. Rarely am I swayed from whims, but as I made my proposal to [[Quinn|Miss Winters]] tonight, I could feel that something was…wrong in a way I struggle to describe.

I was not finished with our game, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Miss Earnest]]. Should you wish it, I would fervidly continue this chase. Should you *not* wish it, my imperative will remain the same - as I can tell you enjoyed the dance as much as I did, no matter what your words convey.

Yet, I am thrown from my usual confidence by your actions, and I cannot help but reflect upon what it is I truly *want*. You have bewitched my mind, and I see a future I had not previously considered - teasing me on the periphery of my sight like the first trace of sunrise across the lake.

I fear that time has made itself my enemy, for it is likely I will disappoint your expectations when we meet again on the morrow. I merely ask you to remain erstwhile; and not give up upon me. Be as you were today, a conundrum that causes my heart to change its face, just as night gives way to day. 

I rarely mourn the leave-taking of [[Mal|Lady Mal]]’s visiting debutants and desperate, fawning girls, but should you slip through my fingers I fear it will sting far more sharply than any keen blade. The worst part, is that I am aware that it will invariably be my own fault.

Whatever the morning light reveals, I remain ever your rose and the thorn in your side;

### Mr. Thomas Hales
