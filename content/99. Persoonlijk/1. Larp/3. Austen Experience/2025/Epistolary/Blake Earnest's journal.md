# Miss Blake Earnest

*“There will be some difficulty in our way, [[Mrs. Norris]]… as to the distinction proper to be made between the girls as they grow up… how, without depressing her spirits too far, to make her remember that [[Fanny Price|she]] is not a [[Maria Bertram|Miss]] [[Julia Bertram|Bertram]]… they cannot be equals. Their rank, fortune, rights, and expectations will always be different.”*
[[Sir Thomas|Sir Thomas Bertram]] 
\- [[0. Mansfield Park|Mansfield Park]] 

I was orphaned by my poor mother, who left my brother and I when she couldn’t support us any longer. For a long time, we had no home and no outlook on life at all, but that changed when the [[Andie|Springs]] family took me in. My brother was taken in by another family, but I still exchange letters with him. I miss him terribly. 

I had to learn to be righteous, strong and to stand on my own two feet. I could never be allowed a slip in character or judgment, and I was always the one taking responsibility for things – even things that someone else ought to have handled or that should have been done already. Some of my friends call me insufferable, or a bore, or I suspect they just find me annoying. So be it. Someone has to be the person that others depend on, that takes things seriously and that doesn’t squander the life they have been given. I came from nothing, had nothing and now I have carved a life out for myself. 

I owe the [[Andie|Springs]] family as much. But, no woman’s life is complete without a man, and I hope, I am convinced, that my character and integrity will be just right for a proper man out there, somewhere.

I would probably turn down most men, because if my feelings and motivations are not pure, I wouldn’t subject a man to a marriage that does not match the expectations he holds. 

I was never treated well by the family who took me in. [[Andie|Duchess Andie Springs]] could have been like a sister to me but instead resents me. I know I should be grateful, but I have a lot of emotional wounds from the whole family’s treatment of me. Most of it was just small, nagging reminders that I was lesser, that I was not truly a part of the family and that I couldn’t expect much of my life. I would never speak about this, and my guilt over feeling ungrateful or critical of them is often overpowering.

I am to be an example for many of the adventurous or passionate, wild young women – and men, for that matter. I am a great support for those in emotional need, but I tend to put my own needs last. I judge people a great deal, berate them for their choices and act a bit holier-than-thou against the other lady guests who aren’t living up to standards. 

My lonely and distanced disposition might of course change when I meet the right, upstanding man, who will find his way into my heart and ease my troubles. He is out there, I just have to be certain of his affections and of my own.

Someone like [[Sir Robert Milton]] could be appropriate, but [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales]] has also expressed an interest in me. I’ve met him before, and I feel a little flattered over the attention - against my own better judgment.

I must be cautious, for as an orphan of no fortune, many might view me as a convenient stepping stone or a harmless adventure. I must not let my heart imagine futures that are not within my reach. My rank, fortune, rights and expectations simply cannot be overlooked, and I shall have to be sure that I don’t surrender my heart or my reputation foolishly.

Against all expectations, I have been invited to attend [[Mal|Lady Mal de Courcy]]'s annual ball at [[The Park|Longbourn Park]]. Her balls are especially known for ending with the announcements of several engagements, so of course there are high hopes for [[Andie|Duchess Andie]] to secure a reputable match. 

Naturally, I am expected to be a faithful companion to the [[Andie|Duchess]], and I am exceedingly grateful to [[Mal|Lady Mal de Courcy]] for condescending to include me in her invitation. Perhaps, while the [[Andie|duchess]] is otherwise occupied, I might chance to get to know some of the less esteemed gentlemen. 

But I do not - I can not - expect to make a match of my own. I have nothing to offer but a fleeting connection to the [[Andie|dowager Duchess Springs]], and I do not expect [[Andie|Duchess Andie Springs]] to maintain our acquaintance once she finds her lucky gentleman. I have seen what the depths of poverty and despair can do, and I would not impose it on someone whose only crimes were to indulge my affections. And yet I may strive to make some lasting connections, that I might have more friends to impose upon when I have need of residence. Or indeed, perhaps [[Mal|Lady Mal de Courcy]] should like me for a companion?

---

We have barely even arrived, and many of the ladies are already misbehaving. Those of us with little fortune and few connections were permitted a brief moment to acquaint ourselves with a few of the gentlemen that [[Mal|Lady Mal]] has so graciously invited. We were the last to arrive and the first to be escorted back to our rooms. But the vast majority have snuck off to play cards in the gentlemen’s parlour. I almost let them drag me along, until my senses returned to me. I fled back to the ladies’ parlour. 

What has come over me? I should not forget my place. I must always keep my wits about me. I cannot risk my reputation. 

Before we were escorted back, I did meet two gentlemen over a game of cards, together with [[Skyler|Miss Wright]]: [[Mr William Lindsey]] and [[Mr George Hawthorne]]. As I was partnered with [[Mr William Lindsey|Mr Lindsey]], we needed to establish a connection quickly. We played Spades, and although my hand was full of clubs, I made a point to rid myself of the red suits first. And [[Mr William Lindsey|Mr Lindsey]] was the first to play a spade, to a call of clubs. So I took the next trick and then made sure to play clubs. He then opened with hearts, which I had run out of, so I took the trick with spades. And clubs again. Back and forth we went. “Clubbing,” he called it. We made quite the team. But after our return, [[Skyler|Miss Wright]] confided in me that she and [[Mr William Lindsey|Mr Lindsey]] have a secret understanding. I must start over.

[[Kamryn|Miss Kamryn Emerald]] has taken it upon herself to call herself my friend. She believes me much too demure and sacrificing. I hardly think I am acting inappropriately, but perhaps she is right in that it does little to recommend me to the gentlemen. She urged me to write a letter to my dream gentleman, if such a man exists, and sign it with my own name. I assented, if only because [[Andie|Duchess Andie]] had reproached me for not practicing my penmanship. I do not think the letter does much to recommend me, either. I left it on the table, and after all the ladies had left, it still lay there, as neglected and forgotten as its author. 

I suppose this is truly what it feels like, to be a woman with no prospects and no accomplishments. I imagine that in all of history, and all of future besides, there are stories such as mine. Tales of childhoods spent fighting for survival, left in the cracks of proper society, unable to call on anyone for help. Their few friends removed without a trace, the very memory of them damaged beyond repair. Snatched up by powers far greater than them, and finally shown kindness; but their kindness a blade that cuts at both ends. Praise our benevolence. Admire our condescension. Be grateful to receive your penance. I was a fool to hope that this event might be any different.

How quickly the winds do change! I was sitting in the shade under a tree, when none other than [[Andie|Duchess Andie]] and [[Quinn|Miss Quinn]] happened upon me. [[Quinn|Miss Quinn]] quite insisted on fetching me a gentleman to attend me for a walk. [[Mr Frank Hartford|Mr Hartford]] was a lovely gentleman with an eye for poetry. A gentle soul who hopes to fill his home and heart with a kind lady with whom to build a family. He was gracious enough to introduce me to [[Captain Thomas Graham|Cpt Graham]], a somewhat shy young man who assured me that the company of quite so many ladies can be quite overwhelming to some of the gentlemen as well, and I should feel quite at ease to tell them off whenever I should need some space. Upon departing, he warned me against [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Thomas Hales]].

Scarcely had I removed my shoes, or [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|the devil himself]] came to invite me to a walk by the wayside. We had shared some glances in the past, but now [[Captain Thomas Graham|Cpt Graham]] was able to introduce us. Our conversation was strangely invigorating. We talked of games, hunts and chases, and I am ashamed to say he enticed me to reveal more of my past than I have ever dared to unveil. Not only that, but he was far from repelled by my past sins. Indeed, he was enraptured! He is eager to see if I could be encouraged to seize whatever should strike my fancy, and indeed challenges me to return [[Andie|Duchess Andie]]’s schemes with feints and strikes of my own. The game is afoot; he has given me a token of affection as our first move. 

While I sit here, [[Mr Frank Hartford|Mr Hartford]] is a wonderful companion, reading and enjoying the countryside. Perhaps a quiet dream is not too far to reach.

This is absolutely preposterous. We were sitting calmly, enjoying the sights and discussing some various dances, when one of the [[0. Company|misters Price]] urges us to go to the lake for some nature viewing. Our group gains in number while some confusion arises as to what we are meant to view, when poor [[Captain John Eaton|Cpt Eaton]] marches down the pier. He rubs his face, seeming to be gathering courage, and proceeds to undress himself. Fully. Only his briefs are ~~perm~~ left on before he dives into the lake to squeals of delight from the ladies around me. Frankly I find it insulting that anyone thought this to be an appropriate outing for a group of unmarried young ladies, and that none of them had the decency to act the least bit appalled or even concerned for the captain’s wellbeing. I left them all, shocked at the sheer impropriety of the entire display. It is an insult and a sacrilege and I can only hope that [[Jane Austen|our Creator]] is not watching from above, as I am sure that [[Jane Austen|our Creator]] would be even more horrified than I am.

Idiot. I am an idiot. At dance practice, I was feeling bold and dared to steal into the dance with [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]]. He was positively thrilled and declared that no one had ever stolen ***him***. But the person I stole out was [[Daya|Daya Amber]], and the sugar sweet tone in which she asked if any of the gentlemen had caught my attention during refreshments tells me that she will be ***trouble***. 

And yet I must admit that [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] is a most lively dancer. Another surprise is [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Mr Hearst]], who is remarkably light on his feet. [[Quinn]] is being very forward, but such is her [[0. Gold|privilege]]. [[Captain John Eaton|Cpt Eaton]] is forward in a more dancing manor: he introduces Viennese waltz grips. Very commanding dancer. [[Mr Richard Durham|Mr Durham]] also seems to know what he’s doing, but he is quite spoken for.

I’ve had the extraordinary pleasure of dancing with [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] again, after stepping in as a gentleman myself for a few dances in order to lay low and not ruffle any more feathers. He really is delightful to dance with, and afterwards he made quite a sport of trying to figure me out. The clues I gave him only confounded and intrigued him more. I am playing a dangerous game with this one; I mustn’t let him goad me into doing something truly reckless. Unlike some, I cannot continue tomorrow as if today did not occur. Although a suggestion from [[Mr Frank Hartford|Mr Hartford]] comes to mind: what if I aspired to be a novelist? Perhaps I could one day adapt my diary into a great three-volume novel, like the ones [[Cecily Cardew|Miss Cecily Cardew]] so eagerly devours. But perhaps, unlike [[Miss Prism]]’s abandoned manuscript, it would not follow the rules of Fiction. The Good need not be happy, and the Bad need not end unhappily.

I was interrupted from assisting [[Varuna|Miss Varuna]] with her dress, when my roommates and I were summoned outside by [[0. Company|representatives]] of [[Mal|Lady de Courcy]]. As [[0. Bronze|ladies of low prospects]], we are not expected to pursue any prospects of rank or fortune, or indeed any gentlemen of interest to ladies of rank or fortune. Frankly, I do not feel any of these reprimands were addressed towards me. In fact, [[Captain Charles Branson|Cpt Branson]], who was witness to our reprimand, was in agreement. I left quite in high spirits, because they made it quite clear that any amusements we enjoy must be scraped together by force. 

Challenge accepted. 

---

All four of us were escorted to dinner by [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Reverend Hearst]], who tried his very best to produce two extra arms for us, but not before all the other ladies had been extensively gathered, dressed, and rushed out the door. [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Mr Hearst]] proved a stimulating dinner partner, and keenly observant of his surroundings and his fellow gentlemen. We battled over the right to fill my glass of water, and I was myself much too self-sufficient for his liking. 

[[Skyler|Miss Wright]]’s clumsiness struck again. After she broke a glass during our first night here, she dropped a knife today during dinner. [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Mr Hearst]] proved similarly clumsy and threw his water glass over [[Skyler|Miss Wright]]. He graciously surrendered his cravat for her to dry herself with. But it seems to have been inspiring, because almost immediately after, [[Doctor Walter Branson|Dr Branson]] spilled his cup quite intentionally in the direction of [[Sam|Miss Sam Steadfast]], smiling all the while. They both left the dining room at that, and [[Rory|Miss Rory Steadfast]] rushed after her to help. [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Mr Hearst]], quite in a panic after losing half of his dates, ran out as well. 

While our “couple” of five was reduced to two, it was announced that for this event, the gentlemen would be switching places after every course. So [[Skyler|Miss Skyler]] and I guarded the unavailable seats of the [[Rory|Steadfast]] [[Sam|sisters]]. We had not one but two gentlemen rushing to take the [[Mr Samuel Hearst|vicar]]’s seat: [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Thomas Hales]] and [[Mr William Lindsey]], [[Skyler]]’s secret betrothed. After some competition, they offered to let the ladies decide, and [[Skyler|Miss Skyler]] left it to me. I chose [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]]. He proved a great conversationalist once again, entertaining all his charges on the seat overlooking four ladies. While we were speaking, I sometimes noticed our knees brushing, as we were seated rather tightly with me at the end and him at the end. Was I imagining things, or was he doing this on purpose? It seemed to happen at such random moments, during words not even addressed to me. But when I felt his leg ride up and over my knee, I jolted in my seat.
“Are you alright, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|Miss Earnest]]?” he immediately asked, his voice not betraying the slightest culpability. I assured the [[Rory|conce]][[Sam|rned]] [[Skyler|ladies]] - and [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] - that I was quite alright, but after a moment’s consideration excused myself to the restroom. 

Upon my return, [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] had also recused himself to use the restroom, and the main course had arrived on various dishes. Left unattended, the [[Rory|Steadfast]] [[Sam|sisters]] and [[Skyler|Miss Wright]] had scarcely managed to gather a few scraps to their plates. 

I had had enough. I marched over to another section of the long table, grabbed a dish, and served it out to our group, including the absent [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]]. Then I retrieved a different dish. And the next. By the time I sat down, we could all enjoy a generous portion of everything on offer.

When [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] returned to find his plate already filled, his clandestine administrations picked back up, though never to those heights as which had made me abandon my seat. I must admit that I made no effort to tell him off. Instead, I prompted him to reveal more of his character. 

---

[[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] lost his parents when he was still very young. He remembers very little of them. He was lucky enough to be taken in by an old former officer, who provided him with stability and education, if not the most caring environment. This officer has since passed away and left his estate and income to young [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]]. He has an adventurous spirit, loves a good chase and loathes to turn down a challenge. He finds it difficult to believe in forever; all things bore and all things fade, and as such he has been a frequent attendee to balls such as this one without finding anyone to share his name with. Above all, he values his agency. The ability, and the courage, to dare to dream, to wish, to desire… and ***take*** it. 

Which is probably why he was so thrown off when [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/2025/Blake|his latest mark]], an overwhelmed wallflower, lowered her guard in his presence and boldly declared that she had only been caught once, and she did not intend to ever let it happen again. That she had been caught by an officer of the law. That she had been carrying a mysterious package with unknown contents. Or when she entered the dance practice by stealing him, only to spend the next few dances competently leading other ladies around, and admitting to participate in staff-only dances. This woman who seemed to relish in being chased, only to turn around and prove an even greater puzzle. A never-ending adventure, there for the taking. 

---

All things end, as did the main course, and it was only fair that [[Mr William Lindsey|Mr Lindsey]] send off [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] to some new spot at the table. [[Skyler|Miss Skyler]] and [[Mr William Lindsey|Mr Lindsey]] had renewed their secret engagement, and were making no efforts to hide their affections from their table companions. [[Sam|Miss Sam Steadfast]] had also become engaged, despite the [[0. Company|Prices]]’ attempts to keep them apart. So when it was announced that after dessert was concluded, the [[Mr Samuel Hearst|vicar]] would be available to perform marriage ceremonies, we schemed to get our two pairs of lovebirds married before any of the [[0. Company|Price family]] could interfere. We snapped up [[Mr Samuel Hearst|Reverend Hearst]] and snuck him out the kitchen doors. 

There was a beautiful outdoor ceremony under an arch in the castle garden. This candlelit joining of this man and this woman, and this man and this woman, has been etched into my memory and I shall replay the scene in my mind for years to come. 

![[double wedding.mov]]

After dinner, the guests gathered for a game of Hide and Seek. It would be carried out in pairs of men and women, their wrists tied together with a piece of ribbon, hidden throughout the gentlemen’s manor, hunted by a few seekers. [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] was eager to verify that I was among those intending to play. Yet when [[Quinn|Miss Winters]] asked him to be her hiding partner, he found himself compelled to acquiesce. 

I decided to sit out the game and update my journal instead, not the slightest bit upset about being left behind. The whole evening had turned into such a comedy of manners, with scarcely hidden trysts in every nook and cranny of the house while the [[Mr Samuel Hearst|vicar]] performed one elopement after another, that I couldn’t help myself but be reminded of our only arriving here the previous evening. 

When [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/3. Austen Experience/Characters/Heroes/Gentry/Mr Thomas Hales|Mr Hales]] and [[Quinn|Miss Winters]] returned to the parlour, having been found, both were looking rather dishevelled. Although [[Quinn|Miss Winters]] made a token remark on how dearly she wished it had been too dark to identify her in their compromising location (the [[Mr Samuel Hearst|vicar]]’s bed) and position (entangled).
