# Mr Thomas Hales
**Group:** 
Gentry Hero

**Income:**
1000 a year

**Austen quote:**
“He had such a degree of lively talent and comic taste, as were exactly adapted to the novelty of acting.”

---

## Tragic Back-story
Growing up without parents leaves a mark in every orphan. You were spared the ravages of poverty when your uncle, an admiral in the royal navy, adopted you as his protégé. Still, the lack of vital emotional support at a young age has shaped who you are. Far from a model father figure, the admiral frequently enjoyed the company of his bottle and associated with disreputable women, all while keeping up outward appearances. Now you have inherited his estate and fortune. The admiral is gone, but you still recognize his voice in your own conversation.

## Description
You are charismatic and can capture an audience with a phrase, a speech or a charade. As this natural actor, you use wit and charm to win favours and affections with men and women. However, you are also egotistical and self- aggrandizing, which some might find rather off-putting. You do love drama in every shape and form, which means you are the first to remark on a friend’s questionable choice of romantic interest and may even challenge someone to a duel over some perceived slight.

## Deep, Dark Secret
You are terrified of committing to a serious relationship. You prefer to act out shallow spectacles of courtship, where you run no risks of being abandoned. You lack maturity, emotional depth and have left several women heartbroken.

## Useful quotes
“I feel as if I could be anything or everything.”

“I have, I flatter myself, made no inconsiderable progress in her affections.”

“I could not preach, but to the educated, to those who are capable of estimating my composition.”

## Plot
You want to be admired and in the centre of attention. Perform an elaborate scene of courtship, inspired by some Shakespearean drama. Cause a scandal and elope with some easily seduced partner. Swear your love and fidelity to several women - particularly poor ones that cannot cause harm to your reputation.  
  
Miss Rory Steadfast and Miss Blake Earnest are two ladies that you’ve met in the past, and you have intentions upon both of them. When it comes to Miss Blake Earnest, you’ve felt an uncomfortable affection after just having met a few times. What does it mean?  
  
You also have a group of friends among the local gentlemen. Or, ‘friends’ more like it.

Richard Durham is a cheerful, simple man in your eyes. He’s an acquaintance but not much more. 

George Hawthorne is someone that is easily provoked into making a fool out of himself by urging him to compete, prove himself or show himself more capable than other gentlemen. Show everyone that you are better than him.

Robert Milton is of high status and wealth, which means he’s earned some kind of respect from you. You need to be careful with men like him. He’s also an old acquaintance. 

Charles Clifton is a brooding gentleman who often withholds affection but not judgment. You don’t mind him so much, and the two of you have known each other for a long time.
