# Sir Robert Milton
You are the image of an aristocratic *bon vivant*, using your father’s carefully managed funds to enjoy life to the fullest. People gravitate naturally to you because of your carefree and charitable attitude.  You are especially fond of gambling and card games, which tend to pre-occupy an ever growing portion of your evenings. You know your way around the social mores of the aristocracy and gentry like the inside of your palm. You evade any questions towards the propriety of your extravagant lifestyle with dismissive superiority.

There has never been a day in your life where you have had to endure the lack of material possessions. You celebrate every morning with the cracks of hunting rifles, every evening with the clinks of glasses, every night with whispers muffled by elegant tapestries in long, winding mansion corridors. You live on the pinnacle of society and should count yourself extraordinarily lucky. However, luxury has not satisfied your hunger for excitement, to the great consternation of your family. To you, life is but a scramble for the next fresh thrill.

Enjoy fine wine and food. Give foolish advice to the aspiring gentry. Try to loosen up a stiff gentleman or lady. Arrange a card game or gambling table and try to recoup your past losses. Find a solution to your brother’s most unfortunate predicament.
