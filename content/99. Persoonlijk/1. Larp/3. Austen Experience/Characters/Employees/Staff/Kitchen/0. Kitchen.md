# Kitchen staff
The Kitchen Staff often have experience from service jobs outside of [[The Park]], and have sought out employment in [[The Park]], which might be a glamorous step up for them. They do menial tasks behind the scenes and are supposed to be seen and not heard, though that is very often not the case. Some of the Kitchen Staff are [[Androids]], who have been brought in as an extra pair of hands. In general, they are treated with civility, but some of the [[0. Staff|Staff]] have certain antipathies towards them and try to lump over their tasks on them.
