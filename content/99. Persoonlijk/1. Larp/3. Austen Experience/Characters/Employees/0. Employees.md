# Employees
There are humans working in [[The Park]] in different positions – as [[0. Kitchen|Kitchen staff]], [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] and of course [[0. Company|Company]] and [[0. Park|Park employees]]. In general [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] shouldn’t be played by [[Humans]], but sometimes the people in question have some sway that has guaranteed them their position.

The [[0. Company|Company employees]] are just that – employees of the [[0. Company|Company]] who are working in the Park, perfecting the [[0. Heroes|Heroes]]. 

[[0. Park|Park]] and [[0. Kitchen|Kitchen]] [[0. Staff|staff]] are also employed by the [[0. Company|Company]], but a different division that is not as high-status. Their focus is on tending to the estate and keeping things running smoothly behind the scenes.
