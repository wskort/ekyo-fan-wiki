# Company
The Company Employees at [[The Park]] are tasked with working with the [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] of [[The Park]], making sure that their performance is perfect and that they deliver memorable stories, swoon-worthy scenes and a happy ever after to the [[0. Heroines|Heroine]]. The Company Employees are divided into two departments: Narrative Design and Behavioral Design. 

However, these structures are not how it always works. Company Employees can meddle in things outside their main area of expertise, with [[0. Behaviour|Behavioral Design]] altering storylines, and [[0. Narrative|Narrative Design]] reprogramming [[0. Heroes|Heroes]]. Usually there are very few repercussions, as long as the Employee doesn’t get caught.

The two Department Heads are mid-level bosses, with very little real power outside of [[The Park]]. They are in charge of structure for each department, making sure that everything that needs to be done is done. They also have some evaluation meetings together. Where standard Employees are often specialised in some ways, Department Heads are supposedly experts in all areas, which means they also take on specific [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] but don’t need to be limited to a certain type of [[0. Heroes|Hero]].

There are also general staff members: lower ranking Company employees who can act as assistants, and fill in when someone is unavailable. Menial tasks and data analysis usually fall to them.
