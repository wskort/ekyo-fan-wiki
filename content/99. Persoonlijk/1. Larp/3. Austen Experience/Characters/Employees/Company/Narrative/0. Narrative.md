# Narrative Design
The Narrative Design department is small and focuses on storylines for the [[0. Heroines|Heroines]], making sure that they all get their happily ever after. The [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] report on their progress to them and the department gives input on how they should proceed. They write up reports advising the [[0. Behaviour|Behavioral Design]] department on what needs to be done and if any models seem to be glitching.
