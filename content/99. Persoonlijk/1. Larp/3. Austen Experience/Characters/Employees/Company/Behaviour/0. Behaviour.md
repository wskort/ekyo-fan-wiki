# Behavioral Design
Behavioral Design focuses on reprogramming of [[Androids]] and [[AI Units]], and each have a set of [[0. Heroes|Heroes]] that they are in charge of. They receive reports from the [[0. Narrative|Narrative Design]] department about where the [[0. Heroes|Heroes]]’ storylines are headed, and based on this decide how to program the models. They also do tests to further enhance the [[0. Heroes|Heroes]].
