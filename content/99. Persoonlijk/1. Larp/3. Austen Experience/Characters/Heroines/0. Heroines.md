# Heroines
The Heroines are influential people in the real world, with finances and prestige enough to get a ticket for [[The Austen Experience]]. They are often well-educated business-people, who for one reason or another are looking for a love story. [[The Austen Experience]] can be equated with an exclusive retreat that promises to be life-changing.
