Embraced in ancient Persia, est. 1200-1300 BCE. Joined the Romans with his own Cult of Mithras (war god inspired by the man). 

On-and-off ruler of London from the first century onwards. Last entered torpor during the Blitz, and presumed dead at the Fall of London by [[Tweede Inquisitie|Second Inquisition]] in 2012. 

The London [[Camarilla]] goes fully silent after this event, so details are impossible to come by.
