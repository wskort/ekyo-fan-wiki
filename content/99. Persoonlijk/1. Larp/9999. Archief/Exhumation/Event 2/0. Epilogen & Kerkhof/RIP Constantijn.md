[[† Constantijn van Tralles|Constantijn]]. 

You were one of the very few who could withstand the storms of [[† Karl Poningh|our lordship]]. You cared for him when no one dared approach, and calmed him when no one could. You watched over [[Frederik Poningh|his boy]] and saw [[Agnes Poningh|my little girl]] grow up. When we faced [[† Karl Poningh|his]] wrath, you were there to get us back to work. You held vigil over [[† Corneel]] when his injuries proved fatal. 

And all this time, I though you indifferent to our plight. So far above us all, with your books and your learning and your expertise and [[† Karl Poningh|his]] favour. I never knew that the same secret that festered in me, had taken root in you. For decades you guarded my deepest shame and [[Agnes Poningh|my daughter]]'s innocence. 

Did the sight of the [[† Catharina Poningh|Baroness]] break you, as it broke me? Was it [[Frederik Poningh|Frederik]], trying so hard to break the cycle that had destroyed [[† Karl Poningh|his father]]? Or was this your final act of defiance against a [[† Karl Poningh|cruel master]]? Was it your revelation that pushed [[Frederik Poningh|Frederik]] over the edge, and drove him to kill [[† Karl Poningh|his own father]]? 

I know you regretted your words the moment you spoke them. But you spoke them all the same. You begged me for forgiveness, and I didn't say a word. Your soothing tonic had such a hold, I barely noticed your passing. I couldn't tell you it wasn't your fault.

I still don't think I can. 

I'll miss you all the same.
