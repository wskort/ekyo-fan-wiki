# Mr Robert Williams (valet)
*The ambitious but under-stimulated valet*

*“There is a stubbornness about me that never can bear to be frightened at the will of others.*  
*My courage always rises at every attempt to intimidate me.”*
– Jane Austen, Pride & Prejudice

[[Mr Robert Williams]] is so happy to be at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. He's waited for his trial by fire since becoming a valet for the [[Johnson household]] and finally it's here. He knows that he's expected to serve [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] and [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]], but he also knows there will be ample chances to prove himself as a competent servant and perhaps to find a pretty girl to flirt with. He's excited, jittery, and so eager to make a good impression. What he fears most is that [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] will think he has done a bad job, and that he might lose his promotion because of it. It's a trial period after all.

### About
[[Mr Robert Williams]] was recently promoted to valet, and has worked for the [[Johnson household]] for years. He is an ambitious young man who is always eager to learn more, and has a winning smile and easy charm that make him well-liked. During the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] he looks forward to enjoying himself and serving the [[Johnson household]] so well that he will be able to remain a valet permanently.
## Who you are
You’re cocky, brash, and charming. Even though you have learned much and behave well enough to have been promoted first to footman, then to valet, you haven’t changed completely from the boy you once were. It is not so noticeable when you put on your best behaviour to impress your betters. However, when you get a bit relaxed and comfortable – now that’s a different story. Then, the London urchin is not so far from the surface.

You think you know a lot more than you actually do. This casual, youthful arrogance colours a lot of your interaction with people. You state things as if you knew they were absolutely true, and you rarely reflect on the fact that they aren’t. Where you got this confidence is somewhat unclear, but you’ve been proven right over and over again in having it. Without the conviction that you could do more than what your jealous friends and family said was possible, you would never be where you are now. 

As much as your overconfidence has helped you, it is also your weakness. You do not know when to quit, you just keep pushing without much thought about the potential consequences. When you’re on a hot streak in cards, you raise the stakes. When you’ve gotten your pay, you spend and feel generous, until you don’t have anything left for the second half of the month. When a drink is offered, you always accept. When a girl gives you a smile, you treat it as an invitation to something more. So far, the consequences have never been dire enough to give you pause, or made you reconsider your choices.
### Secrets
[[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] has requested that you act as his eyes and ears during the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], that you find out as much as possible from the other servants about the other guests. [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] would of course not put it as bluntly as that, but you understand his meaning well enough – he wants a spy, and you have every intention of succeeding in this task. You will not only use your charm and easy manners to make the other servants talk, you are also not above eavesdropping upstairs, or using other means to find useful tidbits for [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]]. If you provide him with useful information, he is sure to be impressed and want you to continue as his valet. Besides, knowing things about people can be useful for you, too.

[[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Miss Matilda Johnson]], the younger of [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]]’s daughters, is quite friendly towards you, and converses with you with more ease and familiarity than is common between upstairs and downstairs people. As a footman, you were often sent to accompany her on errands and visits, and she became increasingly friendly towards you. You quite appreciate this positive attention as it confirms your sense of importance. You maintain the necessary levels of propriety – even you are not so stupid that you can’t see the risks of taking liberties with the young ladies of the house – but you never discourage her behaviour, and always bring out your most winning smile when she is around. 

You are aware that [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|your master]] is violent towards his family, especially [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|his wife]]. From time to time, he will be deeply jealous, and he has had you report on her comings and goings then.
## Your Background
You do not talk much about where you come from, and when you do, you usually lie. You tell people that your father is a farmer in the countryside, but the truth is you never knew your parents. You were raised by your aunt in the poorest parts of East London. This upbringing, this life, has shaped you. It gave you rough edges, but also made you resourceful, witty, and determined to succeed. 

From a very young age, you were expected to earn money for the household, same as everyone else. You were not very strong, nor good with your hands, so you didn’t manage to get work at a factory. But you could run fast, and errand boys could often earn a few pennies. Especially, as it turns out, if you managed to be picked to run a message by one of the important, well-dressed gentlemen in the wealthier parts of town. So, you started spending your time downtown whenever you could, tried to appear clean and trustworthy despite your worn clothes, and slowly built a reputation of being reliable, clever, and a fast runner. 

One of the gentlemen who often sent you to run messages was [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Percy Johnson]]. Sometimes he did not send you to run messages, but had you do other tasks. One time, for example, he had you watch a gentleman’s house for a whole week, and try to follow the gentleman whenever he went out, to learn what places he frequented, where he took his walks and so on. It was after this venture that you plucked up your courage and asked [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] if he perhaps might have a more permanent situation for you. That is how you became a servant in the [[Johnson household]], and your life changed. 

You started out as a hall boy, doing the heavy and dirty work around the house, as well as continuing running errands for [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] from time to time. But you also applied yourself to learning, polishing off the worst of your rough edges, and making everyone downstairs like you. You did well. After some years, you were promoted to footman. It was the greatest triumph of your life. Now, you got to wear livery. You got to spend more time in the presence of the family, serving their meals and carrying things for them. 

Then, just a few months ago, [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]]’s [[Mr Hughes|valet]] requested permission to marry – he had fallen for and proposed to [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Mrs Johnson]]’s [[Miss Evans|lady's maid]]. [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] didn’t approve, and both [[Mr Hughes|valet]] and [[Miss Evans|lady's maid]] were dismissed, “to marry or not as they pleased”. You saw your chance – you had been trying your best to learn from the valet, and [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] seemed to like you well enough. Still, you could barely believe your luck when [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] agreed. You are still on trial, but you are determined to not let him down.
### Dreams and Fears
You want to win. All your life, you’ve wanted a big, significant proof that you are in fact more than your upbringing. You want to win over those of your own age, those of your profession or just people in general. You want to be acknowledged to have won – people need to see it, understand that you are in fact a success. That is all that matters.

So, your dreams have often been centred around this. You dream about rising further, becoming a butler, or perhaps working in an even grander household – and perhaps even further still. You dream of winning the heart of the most beautiful woman. You can even imagine yourself beating someone in a fight. You just want that feeling of everyone looking at you with admiration, recognising that you are a truly remarkable and impressive man. 

Your fears are related to your dreams – what if they never come true? What if you never reach that point of success where you feel satisfied? What if you never reach a point where the people around you look at you with admiration? What if this is as good as it gets, and you’ve climbed as high as you’re ever going to get? Sure, you have gotten further than anyone from your old neighbourhood, but it is not enough. You cannot settle. 

You also fear that anyone should find out just how poor you were growing up, or about your aunt and how you never knew your parents. The thought makes you irrationally ashamed, almost angry, and you have forbidden your aunt of ever trying to reach you at your place of employment. Whatever happens, you want to draw a strict line between your past and your future.
### Goal
You want to impress [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]], both in your duties as valet and anything else he requires of you. You want to take the opportunity to learn from the other valets present at [[Hawthorne Hall]], but preferably without letting them know how little experience you have. You want to socialise and make yourself well-liked by the other servants at the event, and you want to spend time with [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]], and get to know her better.
### Things to do
- Be too confident and arrogant, boast and comment on people’s shortcomings. 
- Take initiatives for things that will be enjoyable for you and the other servants, especially in the evenings. Play games, do a bit of dancing, or whatever else you can think of. 
- Flirt with [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]], and try to do things to get her attention and admiration. 
- Drink too much and cause a minor scene among the servants.
- Be as excellent, charming and competent as you can whenever the upper class people are around.
## Important relationships
You are a charming, ambitious young man, eager to impress anyone and everyone, and show your worth. Still, you are a young man with a young man’s temper and appetites. Given the chance, you want to enjoy yourself properly, and live life to the fullest. You make friends easily (but do not always keep them), flirt readily, and can be trusted to liven up an evening.

### Household
#### [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Percy Johnson]]
is your employer, and you are very aware of your luck in getting promoted to be his valet. You are keen to please him – always have been. You have known [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] for quite a while, since you were a young lad running messages and errands on the London streets. You do think that [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] has come to trust you and see you as reliable over the years, and that is why he has chosen to make you his valet. Still, in the past you have served [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] mostly unobserved, or at least without being noticed much. Now, for the first time, many of your daily tasks are performed in very close proximity to [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]]’s person, and it makes you nervous. You try to cover your nervousness in different ways, with varying degrees of success.

#### [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Mrs Miriam Johnson]]
is the lady of the house. She doesn’t like you much – never has. [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Mrs Johnson]] wants things in her home to be perfect and polished, and she seems to be of the opinion that no amount of varnish could ever make you shine. She and [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] did not see eye to eye – to put it mildly – about making you his new valet. You don’t think there is much chance to win her over after all these years, and your main goal is to not give her cause to convince her husband to sack you. 

#### [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|Miss Agnes Johnson]]
is ever the correct little lady, and as such doesn’t give you much attention. She also makes sure that her younger sister [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Matilda]] minds her station and does not behave informally around you. For this reason, you don’t really like her. Still, you see it as a challenge rather than a lost cause. You try your damndest to impress her in your service, and to be pleasant, correct and inconspicuously charming. From time to time, you think that you see signs that she is approving of you after all. 

#### [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Miss Matilda Johnson]]
seems to enjoy your company – and is showing it more than most people would consider appropriate, if they saw it. She often smiles prettily at you, talks to you, even jokes and laughs with you sometimes. You enjoy this immensely – it inflates your ego, confirms your idea of being better than your fellow servants. Still, it is not without risk to be on such friendly terms with upstairs people, and you take care that you do not overstep in ways that could give her cause to complain to anyone, and that you are never seen chatting informally by anyone else. 

#### [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Anne Hanbury]]
is a new hire, the new lady’s maid to [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Mrs Johnson]] after the last one was sacked. You found her lovely from the very moment she came into the house, and that impression certainly hasn’t changed. You have tried to make her feel welcome in the household, and are very gratified that she often looked to you with her questions while finding her bearings. You really enjoy how you feel when you are in her company. Of late, you have even daydreamed a little of how it would be being married to [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]]. That, of course, is just an idle dream – seeing as the former [[Mr Hughes|valet]] and [[Miss Evans|lady's maid]] were sacked specifically for wanting to marry, there is no hope whatsoever on that account unless you want to lose your jobs. And you, for one, do not. Not even for a girl as lovely and pretty as [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]]. 

### Other Close Relationships
#### [[2. Mr William Brown|William Brown]] 
is a friend of yours. He is also working as a servant in London – for the [[Claridge household|Claridges]], not very well-respected people, but quite rich. From time to time, you find some time to go drinking together, and one never has a better time than when out and about with [[2. Mr William Brown|Willie Brown]]! Still, he is quite the ladies’ man, and you are not altogether fond of the idea of him spending too much time around [[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]]. He’s told you about [[Miss Sally Brown|his sister]] coming to London and you look forward to meeting her and becoming better acquainted with her.
