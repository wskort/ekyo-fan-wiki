# The Marlowes
The [[Marlowe household|Marlowes]] are three siblings from landed gentry in Sussex. They have been invited to the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] since Miss Marlowe is school friends with [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Miss Price]]. Two years ago, the older Mr Marlowe passed away after a long period of illness, and since Mrs Marlowe has been sickly for many years, the siblings have mostly been in charge of themselves during their youth. They are also close to the [[Claridge household|Claridge family]], where Mr and Mrs Claridge have been almost like parents to them in the past.
