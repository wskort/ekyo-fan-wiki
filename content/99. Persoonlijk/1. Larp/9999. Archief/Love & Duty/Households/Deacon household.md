# The Deacons
The Deacons are the wealthiest family of the ones attending the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]]’s fortune is new money, as his father was a banker who made a number of wise investments. His marriage to the Duke of Somerset’s daughter, [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Lady Caroline]], gave the family name more weight and they are on track to being completely accepted in the upper class within a generation or two. A year ago, the family was struck by tragedy as they lost their son and heir, [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]], to a fever. Now only their daughter, [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]], remains to inherit the vast family fortune. Instead of servants they are bringing a musician and dance instructor to the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]].
