# The Claridges
The [[Claridge household|Claridges]] are one of many wealthy families that’s mostly based in London. However, to the people attending the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], they are most known for the scandal 25 years ago. Mr Claridge, who was the former ward of the now deceased Mr Hawthorne, was set to propose to Miss Margaret Price, but disappeared from the house during the last evening. It turned out that he had eloped with a maid, and he added insult to injury by marrying her. After that he was shunned by his former adopted family and lived exclusively in London. Now, an olive branch has been extended and he has been invited by Mrs Hawthorne, along with his family. 

The [[Claridge household|Claridges]] are also close personal friends of Mrs Marlowe, and consider her children to be like their own.
