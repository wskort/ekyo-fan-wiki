# The Prices
The [[The Price Estate|Prices]] are the third richest family in this part of Hampshire, and are significantly poorer than both the [[The Hurst Manor|Hursts]] and the [[Westborough Park|Hawthornes]]. They have also been struck by tragedy, as [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)]] died before providing her husband with a son and heir, which is a bigger issue since [[The Price Estate|the Price estate]] is entailed. Now, the widower and his daughter attend the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] to look for a spouse each. 
The Prices also secured the invitation of the [[Marlowe household|Marlowes]] to the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], as Miss Marlowe is a school friend of [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Miss Price]].
