# The Hawthornes
The Hawthorne family spans over three generations and is the wealthiest family in this part of Hampshire. However, their reputation has been affected by the behaviour of Mr Jeremy Hawthorne, where gossip about his many affairs with men and women has gotten around. There are clear conflicts within the family, who are all individualists, and they do not stand united at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. Perhaps there is a chance that they can finally unite with a common goal, to secure good matches for James and Joanne Hawthorne.

Mrs Judith Hawthorne lives at Hawthorne Hall with her servants, while Mr Jeremy Hawthorne with family lives at Westborough Park.
