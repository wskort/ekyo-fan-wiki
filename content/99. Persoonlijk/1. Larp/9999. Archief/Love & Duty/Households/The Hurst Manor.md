# The Hursts
The Hurst family also spans over three generations and is the second wealthiest family in this part of Hampshire. Unlike the [[Westborough Park|Hawthornes]], the Hurst family has an impeccable reputation, and since the death of the family patriarch, Frederick Hurst, Mr Charles Hurst has successfully taken over running the family estate. They are very respected by the people around them, and will do anything to protect their reputation. Lately, Mr Daniel Hurst (the young heir) has started training under his father to follow in his footsteps.
