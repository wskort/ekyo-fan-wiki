# The Johnsons
The Johnsons are a family that is mostly based in London, since [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Percy Johnson]] is a successful politician. They also have a house in Surrey, in [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]]'s constituency. They are not landed gentry, and their countryside home doesn't come with an estate, but they are influential and respectable, and are therefore considered good people to know. Like the [[The Hurst Manor|Hursts]], the [[Johnson household|Johnsons]] have an impeccable reputation, only slightly marred by their spirited [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|youngest daughter]] who sometimes verges on scandalous.
