### Miss Alice Grant (dance instructor) 
accompanies the Deacon family to Hampshire to teach dance. She is a dedicated teacher who truly loves her art, and are more than prepared to offer private lessons to those who might benefit from her personal touch. She is in a slightly awkward position – neither servant nor upper class – but looks forward to enjoying herself and making some good connections at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]].

---

[[Miss Alice Grant|Miss Grant]] is a London-based dance instructor who is often employed by different girls’ schools to teach dance. She first got to know the [[Deacon household|Deacons]] because she was employed as a private tutor for the children. [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]] was a natural, while [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]] needed a lot of lessons and quickly forgot anything she had been taught. After that she met the family several times in different social circles, and was happy to accept [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]]’s offer to come and instruct in dance during the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]].   

[[Miss Alice Grant|Miss Grant]] is someone who easily fits in among the upper classes, despite having humble beginnings. She has a knack for observing her surroundings and adapting to them, and is also considered a good conversationalist. 

As a dance instructor she can be a strict teacher, especially to those who show promise. She prefers a firm hand and intimate lessons, where she gets to know the other person as a physical creature. She wants people to feel the music and to obey her call without thinking about it. Absolute obedience. 

She is of course helped in this by many other dance instructors being strict. Otherwise her reputation might have suffered by wagging tongues discussing the fact that she has been alone with quite a few men. But she is clear in her professionalism, and her reputation remains intact. It’s as if no one wants to believe that she would act improperly.
