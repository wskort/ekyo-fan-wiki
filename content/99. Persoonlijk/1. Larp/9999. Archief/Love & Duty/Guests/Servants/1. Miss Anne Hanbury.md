### Miss Anne Hanbury (lady's maid)
is already a bit worried about the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. She has [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|three]] [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|ladies]] [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|to serve]], possibly more if [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Lady Caroline]] also requires her services. It's too much, really. But what can she do about it? She will do her best, offer a smile to everyone she sees, and let her imagination run wild while her hands work. Perhaps there will be a charming gentleman there to take notice of her or a servant to have a fun, innocent flirt with? Perhaps there might even be true love in the air?

---

[[1. Miss Anne Hanbury|Miss Hanbury]] is a new hire and will most likely not be a long-lasting one. Not that there’s anything wrong with her attitude – she’s helpful, enthusiastic and tries her best. The problem is that her best is not up to par. She comes from a large, poor family with very few prospects, and doesn’t really have a lot of options to provide for herself if this job doesn’t work out. 

Among the [[Johnson household|Johnson staff]], many feel that it’s a true shame that she’s not cut out for the job, because she is genuinely a delightful girl with a good sense of humour who cares about those around her. That makes it more difficult to tell her off, or to really push for her firing. She’s not completely incompetent, and her hairstyles are functional, if a little simple. It’s just that she doesn’t really show any progress, and her sense of style is just a little off. If asked to recommend a ribbon for a dress, she’ll often suggest the one thing that really doesn’t go with it and is completely unaware that it doesn’t suit. It makes it difficult to chastise her, because she just doesn’t seem to be completely cut out for this job.

She is expected to serve many ladies at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], which worries her greatly. It’s so much work, and one of the women is a [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|LADY]]. Even Anne knows that she’s out of her depth there. She doesn’t want to embarrass herself by giggling or accidentally tugging at her hair, or anything else that can go wrong. 

She’s also worried that all this work will mean that she barely has time to enjoy herself. She wants to dance and walk the grounds and dream of being in a fairytale, but now it’s sounding like she’ll be on her feet all day and constantly being expected to help. And she wants to help, but she also wants to be distracted. It’ll be hard for her to balance those two. 

One of the few skills she actually possesses is picking up gossip. Not that she’s always aware that that’s what she’s doing, but she asks the other servants about strange pieces of information that she’s heard, therefore spreading it despite not meaning to.
