### Miss Mary Campbell (musician) 
is also a part of the Deacon entourage and will play music during dance practices and balls. She is also Lucy Deacon’s music tutor and has a lot of love for the hopeless young girl. She is convinced that Miss Deacon only needs a bit of love and calm to blossom into an agreeable young woman. Despite this delusion, Miss Campbell is someone who is usually a good judge of character and is considered very agreeable, if a little common.

---

[[Miss Mary Campbell|Miss Mary Campbell]] is an experienced musician, who is based in London where she tutors the children of many prestigious families in different instruments. With the [[Deacon household|Deacons]] specifically she has a close bond as she has spent a lot of time with [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Miss Lucy Deacon]], and has been employed as a sort of companion to her as well. Usually she would have turned away an opportunity like that, but the family paid very well, and she actually grew quite fond of the young girl. She knows that many see [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Miss Deacon]] as a lost cause, but [[Miss Mary Campbell|Mary]] just thinks she needs a loving hand and support to blossom into her full potential. 

[[Miss Mary Campbell|Miss Campbell]] hasn’t adopted a posh accent or upper class airs, despite being in the company of so many esteemed people. Instead she is firmly herself, and lets her common way of speaking create a different kind of relationship between her and her students. She has never had to adapt, and she isn’t about to start now. So what if a curse word is sometimes uttered? Or that these young children hear how other Londoners speak? Because of how competently she teaches, she’s allowed to continue so why wouldn't she?

Now, [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]] has employed her to come and play during the balls at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. He’s a curious fellow, but a very good person to know. She is treated like an equal, and an event like this is a lot of money for very little work. She’s looking forward to her downtime, and to meeting new people. And she feels an obligation to try and help poor [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy Deacon]] survive. This event also provides an opportunity to experience new things, and hopefully also some down-time where she can party, get to know people better and maybe even flirt a little. After all, love isn’t reserved for the higher classes.
