### Mr Francis Deacon
is a man of many interests, that all benefit him in his investments. There’s no one better to ask about the development of roads, current shipping trends, or new inventions, though why any member of the upper class would want to know about that stands to question. He’s a keen musician and someone who has managed to mostly learn the rules of his new class, though he sometimes fumbles and has to be corrected. The death of [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|his son]] has hit him hard, and he has not been the same since. The [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] will in many ways be his return to society after his prolonged period of mourning.

---

[[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Francis Deacon]]’s father was a banker who made a large fortune from wise investments in both stocks and trade. As a nouveau riche member of society, the son was sent to the best schools to give him an opportunity to network and climb the rigid structures of upper class society. 

When [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Francis Deacon]] and [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|his sister]] were invited to the [[Hawthorne Hall|Hawthornes]], 25 years ago, [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|the son]] saw his chance to do just that. He was the perfect gentleman back then, making sure that none of the ladies felt neglected or forgotten. That is, until he managed to snag the belle of the ball, [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Lady Caroline Seymour]], who he quickly married. He also secured a match for his sister to his friend [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]], an up-and-coming politician.

He has since expanded the family fortune by investing in stocks, bonds and trade, and is known for being very focused on his work and his quiet hobbies, rather than being out in society much. It often takes a while for him to warm up to others, and to show off his sense of humour or share the stories of his youth. 

A few months ago tragedy struck the family and [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Francis]]’ only son, [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]], died from a fever. Since then, [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]] has been even more of a recluse, but he’s emerged from his sorrow to attend this event. 

Otherwise grief is his constant companion these days. It can strike at the most inopportune times, and completely wreck his composure. He thought it would be easier to bear by now, but is starting to suspect that it will never truly leave him. However, he knows he has to get [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|his daughter]] married. It’s time, and he wants to be rid of the responsibility so that he can focus on his feelings. Maybe he can find another [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]] among the young men.
