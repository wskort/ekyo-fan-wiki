### Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)
is the youngest daughter of the Duke of Somerset, and as such could have had the choice of almost any gentleman she wanted. The choice of [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]] might in that regard have seemed strange, but she seems to thrive in the marriage. Lady Caroline is an accomplished socialite who is a natural guest at any event in town. She is a charming and intelligent woman who splits her time between London, Bath and Somerset, and lets her daughter accompany her as she passes between seasons.

[[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Lady Caroline Deacon]] is one of the many daughters of the Duke of Somerset. Growing up, she was a part of high society but never felt comfortable there. When she was sent to get her education at boarding school she instead found true friendship and female companionship and it was through these friendships that she ended up being invited to the [[Hawthorne Hall|Hawthornes]] 25 years ago. 

People were surprised when she accepted [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]]’s proposal since it seemed as if she was marrying down, but she seemed happy enough and managed to persuade her father to accept the match. Instead of a dowry, the [[Deacon household|Deacons]] were given a cottage on her father’s estate, which led to speculation that it was [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]]’s large fortune that persuaded her father. 

She has given birth to two children – [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]] and [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]]. A few months ago, [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]] sadly passed away from a fever, leaving the family without a male heir. She grieved him in her own way, but knows there is nothing to be done. She is too old to carry another heir. Instead they are attending the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] to marry off [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|their daughter]]. She also looks forward to reconnecting with their old friends, and mourning the ones they’ve lost.

[[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Lady Caroline Deacon]] is known as an impeccable hostess and someone who seems to have a lot of close female friends. She travels a lot and is often sought after as a dinner guest. A very independent woman who knows what she wants in life and goes after it. She’s also surprisingly humble and sweet when you get to know her, not at all what you would expect the daughter of a duchess to be.
