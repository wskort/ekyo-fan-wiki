### Miss Lucy Deacon 
is an awfully awkward young girl. She seems to be unable to improve and learn the rules of society, and constantly needs to be corrected. She doesn’t speak about the right things, forgets the proper etiquette, and is embarrassingly clumsy, but not in the endearing way that would be beneficial in the game of flirtation. At the same time she is set to inherit [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|Mr Deacon]]’s large fortune of £250 000, so if someone is prepared to put up with a wife they constantly have to manage she would be a good business choice. Her music tutor (and only friend), [[Miss Mary Campbell|Miss Campbell]], also accompanies the family to [[Hawthorne Hall|Hampshire]].

[[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Miss Lucy Deacon]] is the only surviving child of the [[Deacon household|Deacons]] and still mourns [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|her brother]] deeply. They were very alike, and she is painfully aware that everyone would rather that she had died. She wants to make it better somehow, to do what’s expected of her and to make [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|her]] [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|parents]] happy. To make them love her and be proud of her choices. 

Though her grandfather was a man who made his fortune, [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|her father]]’s [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|marriage]] has given them a good position in high society. Up until last year, the goal for [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|the daughter]] was to marry into the landed gentry, to establish the family completely as part of the upper class.
However, the death of her brother, [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas]], has made [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|her father]] start to suggest that his future son-in-law should want to take over the family business – investments in stocks, bonds and trade. Something which might affect who she should marry. [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|Her mother]] doesn’t share that view which has made things complicated.

[[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]] is an insecure girl who is often accused of being overly emotional. She’s not quite as good at navigating society as one would have hoped, considering [[2. Lady Caroline Deacon (née Seymour)|her mother]]’s pedigree. Though she’s had extensive schooling, she’s not very proficient in any arts. In fact she has very little to recommend her, except for the vast fortune that she’s set to inherit. It is, however, a very grand fortune. She’s happy that her only friend is joining her. Technically [[Miss Mary Campbell|Miss Mary Campbell]] might be employed as her music instructor, but they have developed a close friendship, and [[Miss Mary Campbell|Mary]] feels like a safe harbour to [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]]. They have become true friends.

She supposes that she’s supposed to be closer with her cousins, but neither [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|Agnes]] nor [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Matilda]] have ever shown an inclination to have anything deeper than a polite distance between them. So [[4. Miss Lucy Deacon|Lucy]] continues on with her friend [[Miss Mary Campbell|Mary]], and hopes that the people at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] will be kinder than the other people in her life. Perhaps she might actually be able to find a husband here, now that the numbers are even. She’s fine with being someone’s last choice, as long as she’s their choice.
