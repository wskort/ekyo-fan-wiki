### Mr Percy Johnson 
has been lucky in life. His marriage brought a sizable dowry, and his patron provided him with power. However, he has used all of this to his advantage, building contacts and forming relationships that will be to his benefit in London. [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] is a shrewd networker, and is greatly helped in this endeavour by [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|his charming wife]]. Sometimes there appears to be a flash of bad temper behind his polite mask, but it is quickly stowed away, at least in public.

[[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Percy Johnson]] is a Tory politician in the House of Commons, with a number of powerful and influential friends. He’s well-spoken and intelligent, and a keen observer of the people around him. He also has a knack for staying just on the right side of the line, without becoming too boring. He is a strict father, but genuinely seems to care about his daughters, especially the youngest one, and the Hawthorne event is a perfect place to secure good matches for both of them. His marriage also seems solid, and the couple puts up a united front against the world.

25 years ago, it didn’t seem like he and [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Miss Miriam Deacon]] were a natural match. There was a close friendship between the two families, but the engagement was surprising. [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] had been mostly disinterested or distant, struggling a bit to find his place among all of these families that knew each other and he had no clear objective in mind. But it ended with what seemed like a practical engagement, and the two have been mostly inseparable ever since, at least in public. 

Since then, [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] has really grown into the politician he was destined to become. He has the patronage of an influential lord that helped him secure a place in the House of Commons, and he has hope of becoming the Tory whip (the official who ensures that members of the party vote according to the party platform) in the future, or maybe even joining the cabinet. The connections he can make during this weekend could help him get there.

A few months ago there was an unfortunate snag in his great plans for the future. [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|His oldest daughter]]’s [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|fiancé]] passed away in a fever and with that went his potential access to the [[Deacon household|Deacon]] money. Sure, [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|his wife]]’s dowry increased his fortune significantly, but he wants more. He wants [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|his]] [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|daughters]] to secure great matches that will help his own career in the future.
