### Miss Matilda Johnson
has a head full of fairy tales, gothic romances, and whatever her current obsession is. She's fiercely competitive, especially towards her sister, and she so wants to snag the best match at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]]. At the same time she is quite naïve and inexperienced with the world, and could easily end up in trouble. She doesn't always realize the consequences that her actions might have on her reputation. 

---

[[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Miss Matilda Johnson]] is an accomplished young woman with a playful streak. As a younger sister, she’s constantly been compared to [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|her older sister]] growing up. [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|Agnes]] is practically perfect in any way, and [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Matilda]] has always felt like she isn’t measuring up to her. Still, [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|her sister]]’s biggest vice is her competitiveness and [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|Matilda]] enjoys drawing that out of her and competing. And she especially loves the feeling when she beats her sister at something. Those are the small victories that she has in life.

She wants to win at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] too, to get the best marriage and snag the most interesting man. To experience true love and romance. But she hasn’t really considered the actual consequences of a marriage, what happens after the engagement and how permanent a choice it can prove to be. This will most likely start to dawn on her over the course of the coming days.

What her friends know about her is that she loves a good gothic romance and dreams of a passionate and possessive love, like the ones she’s read about. Books are in a way her refuge when things get tough, and the stories she’s read are ones she applies to real-life situations. To understand what’s happening in the world.

She loves [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|her father]] a great deal, though her mannerisms are more reminiscent of [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|her mother]] as a young girl, and wants to impress him. In his presence she tries to put aside all the girlish pursuits she usually has and instead seem wise and sharp. It’s a far cry from the person she usually is among others.
