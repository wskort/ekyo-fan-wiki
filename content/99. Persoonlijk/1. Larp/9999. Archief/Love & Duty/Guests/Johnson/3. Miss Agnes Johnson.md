### Miss Agnes Johnson 
is in many ways the perfect young woman. She's accomplished, polite, and knows all the rules of society. It seemed that her happiness was made as she and her cousin [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas Deacon]] were engaged last year, but it was not to be. He died of a fever, and she remained unmarried. Now, she has just completed another London season without a proposal, and the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]] is likely  her last chance at marriage. She desperately wants to marry, whatever the cost, and is prepared to go to great lengths to reach her goal.

[[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|Agnes Johnson]] is the oldest daughter of the [[Johnson household|Johnsons]]. She’s a refined and accomplished young woman who is just as she should be. She dabbles in the arts, reads, walks and speaks several languages. She speaks and thinks about things that young women should have on their mind, and seldom puts a foot out of line. She’s a pleasant partner, both at dinners and at a dance. The definition of a girl that the older generation praise for her virtues, and that the young men therefore don’t find as interesting. 

She’s gone through several London seasons and is still unmarried. There was hope, as she was engaged to her cousin, [[3. Mr Thomas Deacon|Thomas Deacon]]. However, he passed away from a fever before they got married, and so she is once again without a clear prospect. For every month that passes, she gets older and if she emerges from this event without an engagement, people will most likely start to talk about her being past her prime. She is dreadfully aware of this, just like [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|her]] [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|parents]] are.

[[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|Agnes]] also has a competitive streak that really comes to life if she ever thinks [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|her sister]] is getting something that she wants. Even though she loves [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|her sister]] more than life itself, they constantly compete – in races, in accomplishments and over dance partners, and she really doesn’t like to lose. She wants to be the best at everything, and if [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|her younger sister]] is better then that is no longer true. It is something that sometimes pushes her to do something unwise, which might be exactly what she needs to stop just being a façade and become truly interesting to the opposite sex.
