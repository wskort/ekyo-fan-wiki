### Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)
used to be a more spirited and rebellious young lady, but since her marriage she has settled well into the position of wife and mother. She seems to enjoy throwing parties, and meeting with influential people. Rarely does she seem flustered or overwhelmed. Her brief flirtation with Charles Hurst 25 years ago seems to be long forgotten, and there is absolutely nothing to indicate any regret. Her daughters also appear to be her world, and she is both a doting and strict mother to them.

[[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Mrs Miriam Johnson]] is the picture-perfect wife of a prominent politician, and someone who is used to hosting both charity events and dinner parties. She’s a strict mother to her [[3. Miss Agnes Johnson|two]] [[4. Miss Matilda Johnson|girls]], who seem to both be doing well for themselves in the world even if they’re not married yet. Hopefully this weekend will see to that, as well as allow her to reacquaint herself with her old crowd.

25 years ago, things were very different. It looked like the young [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Miriam Deacon]] seemed to be falling for [[1. Mr Charles Hurst]] and he for her, which meant that her sudden engagement to [[1. Mr Percy Johnson|Mr Johnson]] came as a bit of a surprise to the rest of the group. But from the moment their engagement was announced, she was a devoted partner to her husband-to-be. Something that does not seem to have wavered since.

She used to be wilder and freer. A rebel who pushed the limits of what she was allowed to do. But with her marriage that faded away. Instead she settled into the role of wife, who needed to represent her husband well so as to help his career without a blemish on her own reputation.

Among her friends, [[2. Mrs Miriam Johnson (née Deacon)|Miriam Johnson]] is known for her sparkling wit and ability to plan fun but appropriate games and entertainment. She seems to genuinely care about her friends and offers them distraction when they need it, but tries to keep the mask up even to those who know her best. Only [[1. Mr Francis Deacon|her brother]] has really managed to make it crack in the last few years.
