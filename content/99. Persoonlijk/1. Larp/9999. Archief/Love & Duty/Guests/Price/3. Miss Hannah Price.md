### Miss Hannah Price
is the only surviving child of [[1. Mr Frederick Price|Frederick]] and [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|Martha Price]], and has strongly felt the absence of a mother figure in her life. [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Miss Price]] is a bit too lively and loud, but has rarely been told so. Instead [[1. Mr Frederick Price|her father]] dotes on her and fails to see the issue with having an independent daughter that is barely in need of a parent. Of course, the rules will have to change at the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], or [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Miss Price]]’s prospects may be damaged by her behaviour.

---

[[3. Miss Hannah Price|Miss Price]] knows she is not like girls are supposed to be. She never was.  She feels everything so strongly, and it just gets worse as she gets older. Her whole being is filled with this aching hunger. Always restless, wanting more from life than what she has been given and knowing it is not appropriate to long for it. Her restlessness makes her sometimes forget herself and instead do the most daring, delicious and inappropriate things. Only to feel alive. 
  
She dreams about falling in love and being worshipped. Her longing is physical and she is an aching void just waiting to soak up all the admiration the young men could possibly give her! Ripe to fall in love and be married. She tries to hide these urges of hers of course. It is highly inappropriate for a young woman who wishes to marry. 
  
[[3. Miss Hannah Price|Hannah]] has always been close to [[1. Mr Frederick Price|her father]], especially since [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|her mother]] died five years ago, and it will hurt her to leave him. She understands why he is thinking of remarrying, to not become utterly alone when she is married off and to keep the estate within the family, but it still feels uncomfortable thinking of [[1. Mr Frederick Price|her father]] married to a girl her own age, especially since he seems to assume she will help him woo one of her school friends. At the same time she doesn’t want to disobey him. He loves her unconditionally and has never asked her to change.  
  
[[3. Miss Hannah Price|Hannah]] has always been thirsty for affection, ever since she was a little girl. [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|Her mother]] never cared much for her, and was always out of the house, putting all her time and effort into caring for other people's children, rarely sparing a thought to her [[3. Miss Hannah Price|own daughter]] back home. It has left [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Hannah]] weak as she clings to the people who show her any affection. She is terrified to be left or discarded like [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|her mother]] discarded her. 

At school she got to know [[2. Miss Eleanor Marlowe|Miss Eleanor Marlowe]], someone who has mastered the art of appearing as one thing in public and a different one in private. They became fast friends even though they seemed like an odd match, and now [[3. Miss Hannah Price|Hannah Price]] refuses to let go of her grasp of [[2. Miss Eleanor Marlowe|Eleanor]]. She isn’t going to lose her like she lost [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|her mother]]. [[2. Miss Eleanor Marlowe|Eleanor]] is hers now.
