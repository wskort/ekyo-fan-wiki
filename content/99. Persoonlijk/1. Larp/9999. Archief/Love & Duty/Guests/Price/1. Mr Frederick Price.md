### Mr Frederick Price 
is the brother of Mrs Phoebe Hawthorne and cousin of Miss Margaret Price. He is a man who runs his estate competently, but has a worse reputation in London where he’s been seen intoxicated and coming out of places of ill repute. However, he is a widower with an estate, and the woman who marries him might not have to wait long before becoming a wealthy widow herself.

---

Finally it’s time for [[1. Mr Frederick Price|Mr Frederick Price]] to take charge of his life and do something about his situation. Though his estate is doing fine, and he has [[3. Miss Hannah Price|a daughter]] that’s ready to be married, there are many things working against him. His wife, [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|Martha]], died before producing a male heir. This wouldn’t be as big of an issue if [[The Price Estate]] wasn’t entailed. As things stand right now, it would pass to the closest male relative when he dies, and the whole world would view his life as a failure. 

If only [[2. Mrs Martha Price (née Johnson)|his wife]] hadn’t been sickly for so many years. He wasted so much time waiting for the slow, inevitable end where he should have been courting her replacement. But it’s only been five years. Surely no one would think he’s been too slow? Now that [[3. Miss Hannah Price|his daughter]] is ready for marriage it’s perfect timing for them both to find someone. He wants to accompany her on this journey, as is only fitting for the true woman of his heart. The one person he sees nothing wrong with. His love for her is endless, and he finds no fault in her spirited behaviour. It makes her happy, and that makes him happy.  

He’s had urges before to make things right and to procure a son somehow. To find a breeding mare from a good family and settle down. But every time something has distracted him on his quest. Usually a visit to London and its many entertainments. Sometimes just a few bottles of sherry and a new servant. It just never went from a plan to actual action. In a way he’s grateful for the [[Love & Duty|Hawthorne event]], even if it feels slightly humiliating to be comparing himself to these young, insecure boys, and competing with them over nailing down the best match. Now he’ll actually have to follow through. 

He’s experienced, mature, and knows what class and style is. And he has a lot to offer a wife, as long as she’s fertile. An estate, connections to good families in the neighbourhood, and a comfortable life as a widow once he passes. Why would anyone turn him down?
