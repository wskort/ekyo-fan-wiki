# Ballet dell'Arte
## Cast
### Vecchi 
1. Il Dottore - decadent scholar, mansplainer
2. La Signora - wife of Pantalone, tough & calculating, cougar, hits on Lélio 
3. Pantalone - old scrooge, lustful, frail 
4. Tartaglia - stuttering statesman, lower rank, timid

### Zanni 
1. Pulcinella - lazy schemer, suck-up, turncoat 
2. Pedrolino - ideal servant, lives to serve, haughty 
3. Arlecchino - servant to two masters, opportunist 
4. Colombina - perky maid, cheats on Arlecchino with Pantalone, Il Capitano and Pulcinella 

### Capitani 
1. Il Capitano - proud boastful coward 
2. Scaramuccia - sleezy traitor, mercenary 
3. Yde - overlooked underling, true heroine 

### Inamoratti 
1. Isabella - noble daughter of Pantalone & La Signora (no mask) 
2. Lélio - noble son of Il Dottore (no mask) 
3. Tartaglia (see above) 
4. Yde (see above)
