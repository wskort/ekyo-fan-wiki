# Ballet d'Animaux
Haastig aangepaste variant van [[99. Persoonlijk/1. Larp/1. Belvedere/Kunstwerken/Le Ballet dell'Arte|Le Ballet dell'Arte]]. 

## Cast
### Vecchi 
1. ~~Il Dottore~~ **Beer** - decadent scholar, mansplainer
2. ~~La Signora~~ **Nachtegaal** - wife of Pantalone, tough & calculating, cougar, hits on Lélio 
3. ~~Pantalone~~ **Ekster** - old scrooge, lustful, frail 
4. ~~Tartaglia~~ **Das** - stuttering statesman, lower rank, timid

### Zanni 
1. ~~Pulcinella~~ **Eekhoorn** - lazy schemer, suck-up, turncoat 
2. ~~Pedrolino~~ **Hond** - ideal servant, lives to serve, haughty 
3. ~~Arlecchino~~ **Rat** - servant to two masters, opportunist 
4. ~~Colombina~~ **Poes** - perky maid, cheats on Arlecchino with Pantalone, Il Capitano and Pulcinella 

### Capitani 
1. ~~Il Capitano~~ **Wolf** - proud boastful coward 
2. ~~Scaramuccia~~ **Vos** - sleezy traitor, mercenary 
3. ~~Yde~~ **Zwaan** - overlooked underling, true heroine 

### Inamoratti 
1. ~~Isabella~~ **Witte duif** - noble daughter of Pantalone & La Signora (no mask) 
2. ~~Lélio~~ **Hermelijn** - noble son of Il Dottore (no mask) 
3. ~~Tartaglia~~ **Das** (see above) 
4. ~~Yde~~ **Zwaan** (see above)
