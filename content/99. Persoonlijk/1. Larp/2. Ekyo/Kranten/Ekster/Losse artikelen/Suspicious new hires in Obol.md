## Suspicious new hires in [[Obol Corporatie|Obol]] 
Recently [[Obol Corporatie|Obol Corporation]] has hired new staff to work on the [[Eiland|recently discovered island]]. However, it has come to my attention that people have different intentions than originally presented. Here are some of the suspicious findings: 

**[[Quentin Starling|Starling]] and [[Markos Erikson|Erikson]]:** what are they actually up to? These men have not rolled up their sleeves to actually do anything for [[Obol Corporatie|Obol]] while on the [[Eiland|island]]. Where did they get their money? They only spend their time in the tavern, chatting to the staff. Why come when you won't work, truly unbelievable. So what are their true intentions? Do they have other connections? Or do they work for a certain [[IJzeren Karavaan|criminal organization]]?
 
**[[Fidelis Petramagus|Petromaris "Peet"]]:** The transferred [[Parageologie|para-geologist]] from the [[Orde van het Heilig Akkoord|Heilig Akkoord]]. At first glance, he is a gentle guy with his [[Peet's staf|glowing stick and crystal]]. But we need to look deeper into this. Why the sudden transfer? Did he move because of differences, or is he a SPY??? Are we sure he is trustworthy to work for [[Obol Corporatie|Obol]]?

**[[Barnabas Redd]]:** The silent mechanic who always stands on the side. Seemingly blends in with the rest of the crew, like he isn't someone. But there is something up his sleeve and no one notices. He slips away when no one is watching, but I am. What are his true intentions? What is he up to? And are his loyalties even to [[Obol Corporatie|Obol]]? If not, then who?
