# Aaron Santos
*Corporate. Loyalty 1, Connection 1.*

Aaron Santos is a former student of [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]]’s from back when she was teaching martial arts to local kids. In his younger years, Aaron was a talented athlete, particularly skilled in parkour and martial arts, with dreams of going pro in extreme sports. He was a dedicated student, quick on his feet, with a natural competitive edge and an admiration for [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]]’s skill and discipline. Aaron used to look up to her not just as a mentor but as a role model, drawn to her unique style and sense of honor, even in the rough parts of town. Despite [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]]’s best efforts, though, life eventually pulled him in a different direction. Now, Aaron is working a low-level corporate job as a junior marketing analyst for [[Evo]]. He feels stifled by the monotony and corporate structure, and he still longs for the thrill of his athletic days. Aaron occasionally reaches out to [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]], reminiscing about the "good old days" and hinting at his dissatisfaction with corporate life. Though he’s not directly involved in anything illegal, he’s often tempted by the excitement that Shadowrunning represents, and it wouldn’t take much to convince him to bend a few rules if it meant getting a taste of the thrill he’s been missing.
