# Jaime Alvarez
*Street, Criminal. Loyalty 6, Connection 4.*

Jaime Alvarez, is [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]]’s ride-or-die from childhood. Growing up together in the rougher neighborhoods of Seattle, they forged an unbreakable bond through endless mock-tournaments, friendly fights, and competitions, turning the streets and alleys into their own playground. Jaime is the kind of friend who would jump into any fight for [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]], and their bond is more like family than friendship. Though they started off on similar paths, Jaime took a different turn as they grew older. Drawn into gang life, Jaime found a sense of purpose and belonging among a crowd that valued loyalty and power — a far cry from the discipline and introspection [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]] cultivated as an adept. Jaime’s life now revolves around the street scene, where gang colors and turf wars are part of daily survival. It’s a path [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]] doesn’t approve of, but she can’t deny Jaime’s commitment or his street smarts; he’s a survivor, albeit one with dangerous connections. Despite his choices, Jaime’s loyalty to [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]] never wavers. When she was trying to find a place among the shadowrunners, he was the one to make the introductions, calling in a favor from [[Zazzalil|Zazzalil]] to give her a shot. It was a way of repaying a debt to [[Zazzalil|Zazzalil]] and of proving to himself that he could still look out for [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]], even as his own life was spiraling. Jaime has a streetwise charisma, drawing people in with an easy smile and quick wit, but there’s a hardened edge behind it — he’s been around long enough to know when to watch his back, and he’s not above using [[Hiraya Gecko Mendoza|Gecko]]’s skills for his own purposes.

Jaime is a solidly built ork with a slight swagger that gives him a certain charm. He has tattoos running down his forearms, intricate designs inspired by Filipino tribal art mixed with his gang’s symbols, serving as both a badge of honor and a warning. His black hair is cropped short, his expression sharp, and his gaze always intense, scanning his surroundings with a mix of alertness and caution. His wardrobe consists of rugged streetwear — jackets with gang colors, old jeans, and heavy-duty boots.
