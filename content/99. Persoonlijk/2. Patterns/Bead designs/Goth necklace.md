# Goth necklace
![[necklace1.jpg]]
![[Pasted image 20231011173559.png]]
## Materials
- 3mm round (faceted)
- 4mm round (faceted)
- 5mm round (faceted)
- 6mm round (faceted)
- 8mm round (faceted)
- 7x5mm pears (faceted)
- Size 10 or 11 rocailles / seed beads

## Construction
### Doll shape
#### First strand
For the 'doll' shape, start with a strand like this on a 1m thread with a needle on each side. It's a two-way weaving pattern, going across through the middle bead on each turn. 
![[Pasted image 20231011145254.png]]
#### Basic oval
Add a second strand with overlapping tips to make an oval. 
![[Pasted image 20231011145605.png]]
#### Finished oval
Thread back down and splice in 4 seed beads per side.
![[Pasted image 20231011150033.png]]
#### Tear shape
For the top of the oval, the inside beads stay (relatively) small and the outside beads get progressively larger. Again, add in 4 seed beads per side on a return trip. Tie off. 
![[Pasted image 20231011150226.png]]

You have completed the 'doll' shape! You need 5 of them. 
### Flower shape
FPP = pear-shaped bead
R = rocaille = seed bead

Each pair of pears touches with the narrow end, with 4 seed beads between the wide ends. There are 8 pears in total. 
![[Pasted image 20231011152132.png]]

You need 4 flower shapes, 1 to go between each pair of doll shapes. 

### Stringing together
#### Connecting the dolls
Start with 1m thread and needles on both ends. 

String 16 seed beads, then feed both needles through the 5mm bead of a doll head. Pass needles through to the mirror end. 
![[Pasted image 20231011154439.png]]
![[Pasted image 20231011153227.png]]

Now we get the middle motif with 8 seed beads per thread, then a motif of 4mm beads (more at the top than at the bottom), and another 8 seed beads per thread. 
![[Pasted image 20231011154530.png]]

Connect to the next doll shape, etc, end with another ring of 16 seed beads. Tie off. 
#### Adding the flowers
Start with 1m thread and needles on both ends. 

This time we start at the center of the dolls, connecting to these beads here:
![[Pasted image 20231011155518.png]]

String 8 seed beads per thread, then thread through a connecting 4 seed beads in the flower, pass through and add a 4mm bead to the center, pass to through to the other side, another 8 seed beads per string, and attach to next doll. 
![[Pasted image 20231011155747.png]]

Repeat for all flowers & dolls. Tie off. 
![[Pasted image 20231011155315.png]]
#### Bottom connecting row
Start with 1m thread and needles on both ends. Thread 16 seed beads and connect to first doll at the middle of the oval shape. We're going to make an X shape with seed beads inside the oval shape. Image shows shape, NOT AMOUNT. Depending on your size of seed beads, you'll need 13-15 for one string and then 6-7 for the second, connect to center, and another 6-7. 
![[Pasted image 20231011161539.png]]

Now we get a variation on the middle motif with 8 seed beads per thread, then a motif of 4mm beads (more at the bottom than at the top), and another 8 seed beads per thread. 
![[Pasted image 20231011162243.png]]

Continue to connect all dolls together. End with another loop of 16 seed beads at the outer side of the final doll. Tie off.
### Final accents
#### Danglies
##### Between dolls
Here we use straight pins and/or jump rings, the pear-shaped beads and the 6mm beads. Attach between the two middle 4mm beads of the bottom connecting weaves. Make 4. 
![[Pasted image 20231011165209.png]]
##### Middle dolls
In addition, we're making 3 long danglies to attach at the bottom of the non-edging doll shapes, using pear beads, 8mm beads, 3mm beads and 5mm beads. 
![[Pasted image 20231011171440.png]]
##### Edge dolls
Finally, for the edging doll shapes we'll make a simpler version with just a pear bead and a 5mm bead each. 
![[Pasted image 20231011172419.png]]
#### Loops
Now make 3 layers of loose loops of beads between the dolls with whatever you have left and however strikes your fancy. There are no rules. Go nuts. Straightpin-chains are also on the menu, but a pain to fabricate in bulk. 

Pictured below, there seems to be a pattern of 3 seed beads and a larger bead in sequence, where the larger bead is sequentially larger towards the center of the loop, and the loops are attached to sequential bead spaces in the doll's bottom. Each lower loop contains more repetitions to increase length. 
![[Pasted image 20231011173057.png]]
#### Closure
Make extra bead-chains for length as needed, alternated with jump rings for length adjustability, on one end. Add 2 lobster clasps on the other end. Honestly, she stopped trying to explain anything at this point so have some screenshots.
