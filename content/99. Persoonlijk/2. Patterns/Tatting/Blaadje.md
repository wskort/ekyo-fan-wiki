# Blaadje
![[Blaadje patroon.png|500]]

Start with two shuttles wound continuously.
Only need a small amount of thread on each.
Make all picots very small (they are just for joining).

R5-5, R5-5 dnrw (do not reverse work)
C2, R5-5 rw (reverse work)
R5-5 dnrw
C2, R6-6 rw
R6-6 dnrw
C12 lj (lock join) (to tip of 5th R)
C8 lj (to tip of next R)
C8 lj (to tip of next R)
C4, R4, C4 lj (to tip of next R)
C8 lj (to tip of next R)
C8 lj (to tip of next R)
C12 lj (to base of leaf)

Then continue the chain for as long as you want the stem to be.
