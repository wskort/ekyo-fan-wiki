## Patroon:
Begin with two shuttles, threads unconnected. Tat the first ring with the first shuttle, then take up the second shuttle and tat the second ring around it. This is your first wing. Leave a little bit of space between sets of rings to let the entire onion fit. 
Order of tatting: 
- Ring 1+2 upper right wing
- Ring 3+4 lower right wing
- Ring 5+6 lower left wing
- Ring 7+8 upper left wing
Once round, square knot all four threads together, then knot in pairs of two to make the antennae.
